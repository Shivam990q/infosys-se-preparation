# Mysore GEC Day 1 Induction & Campus Onboarding Protocol

**Location:** Infosys Global Education Center (GEC), Hebbal Industrial Area, Mysuru  
**Role:** Systems Engineer Trainee (JL2) | **Window:** First 5 Days on Campus  
**Significance:** Administrative setup, security protocol, workstation allocation, and corporate acclimatization.

---

## 4. Workstation Allocation & Thin Client Setup

On Day 4, you are assigned an individual workstation inside **GEC-1** or **GEC-2**:

1. **Virtual Desktop Infrastructure (VDI):** Workstations run locked enterprise desktop images. Personal USB drives and external storage are disabled by group policy.
2. **Access Credentials:** You will configure your **Single Sign-On (SSO)** credentials used for Lex, Sparsh, and FA examination portals.
3. **Software Environment Pre-Installed:**
   - Python 3.x, Visual Studio Code, Eclipse IDE / IntelliJ Community.
   - MySQL Server & MySQL Workbench / Oracle Database.
   - Git command line tools and internal Infosys library repositories.

---

## 5. Banking & Payroll Onboarding

On-campus branches of **ICICI Bank**, **Axis Bank**, and **State Bank of India (SBI)** set up specialized corporate account desks:
- Zero-balance corporate salary account opened on the spot.
- Debit card and net banking kit delivered within 48 hours.
- Direct linking of bank account to Infosys Payroll in **Sparsh** for monthly stipend deposit.
