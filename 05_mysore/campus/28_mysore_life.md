# Mysore GEC Campus Life, Hostels, Food & Survival Logistics

**Location:** Infosys Global Education Center (GEC), Hebbal Industrial Area, Mysuru, Karnataka  
**Campus Size:** 350 Acres | **Trainee Capacity:** ~14,000 Trainees Simultaneously  
**Role:** Systems Engineer Trainee (JL2) | **Training Duration:** 14 to 18 Weeks

### Financial Breakdown During Mysore Training:
- **Gross Monthly Stipend:** ~**₹19,500 / month**
- **Accommodation & Maintenance Charges:** ~**₹3,000 – ₹4,000 / month** (deducted from payroll)
- **Food Expenses:** ~**₹5,000 – ₹6,500 / month** (paid directly at food courts via UPI / debit card)
- **Net Surplus / Savings:** Trainees comfortably take home **₹8,000 – ₹10,000 / month** in net savings during training.

### Dress Code Policy:
- **Monday to Thursday (Formal Business Attire):**
  - *Men:* Collared formal shirts, formal trousers, formal leather shoes, and Infosys lanyard with ID badge. (Jeans, round-neck T-shirts, sneakers, and sandals are strictly prohibited in academic buildings).
  - *Women:* Formal salwar kameez, formal trousers with shirts, or formal western suits.
- **Friday (Business Casuals):**
  - Neat polo T-shirts, clean jeans, and smart casual footwear.

## 5. Weekend Outings & Exploring Mysore City

Trainees are free to explore outside the campus on weekends:

- **E-Gate Pass System:** Before leaving campus on Saturday or Sunday, you must log into the intranet portal and apply for an **Outstation Pass** if staying overnight in Bangalore or traveling home.
- **Local Attractions in Mysore:**
  - Mysore Palace (illuminated on Sunday evenings).
  - Chamundi Hill & Temple.
  - Brindavan Gardens & KRS Dam.
  - Quick connectivity to Bengaluru via the 10-lane expressway (Vande Bharat train takes ~1 hr 45 mins).
- **On-Campus Healthcare:** Apollo Clinic operates 24/7 on campus with free doctor consultations, pharmacy, and an emergency critical-care ambulance.
