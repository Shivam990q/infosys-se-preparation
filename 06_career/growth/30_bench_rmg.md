## 3. The Internal Client / Account Interview

Before a client (e.g., Bank of America, Apple, Walmart, Daimler) allows you to touch their code repository, the Infosys Account Lead or Client Architect conducts an internal screening interview.

### What Account Panels Grill Freshers On:
1. **Mysore Capstone Project:**
   - *"Walk me through the exact database schema and API endpoints you built in Mysore FA3."*
   - *"Did you handle exceptions and transaction rollbacks?"*
2. **Live Problem Solving:**
   - Live query: Writing a multi-table `JOIN` with `GROUP BY` and `HAVING`.
   - String manipulation or array sorting in Java/Python without built-in library functions.
3. **Domain Familiarity:**
   - If interviewing for **Financial Services (FS):** Basic understanding of payment gateways, credit card transaction states, and ledger accounting.
   - If interviewing for **Retail (RTL):** Supply chain flow, inventory tracking, order management states.
4. **Shift & On-Call Willingness:**
   - *"This project supports European / US banking hours (2 PM – 11 PM or 6 PM – 3 AM). Are you comfortable?"*
   - *Winning Answer:* An immediate, enthusiastic **"Yes, absolutely."**

## 5. What Happens If You Fail Multiple Account Interviews?

- **1st Rejection:** No penalty. RMG schedules an interview with a different delivery unit within 5–10 days.
- **2nd Rejection:** RMG coordinator meets with you to review interview weaknesses and assigns mandatory refresher modules on Lex.
- **3rd Rejection:** You are categorized as **"Hard to Allocate"**. HR intervenes, and you lose choice over project type—you will be assigned automatically to any available requirement (often legacy maintenance or automated testing).
- *Prevention:* Treat internal interviews with the exact same rigor as your campus placement interview.
