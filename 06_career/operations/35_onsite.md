## 1. When Does an Engineer Become Eligible for Onsite Travel?

Infosys deploys thousands of engineers to client locations worldwide. The eligibility criteria are strictly codified:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         ONSITE DEPLOYMENT ELIGIBILITY                           |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Parameter         | Minimum Benchmark                                           |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Tenure         | Minimum **2.5 to 3 years of continuous service** (typically |
|                   | upon promotion to Senior Systems Engineer - JL3B/JL4).      |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. Appraisal      | Consistent performance ratings of **CR (Commendable)** or   |
|    Ratings        | **EE (Exceeds Expectations)** in the last two annual cycles.|
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. Passport       | Active international passport with minimum **2 years of     |
|                   | remaining validity** and blank visa pages.                  |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. Client Request | Formal request from the onsite Account Client Partner for   |
|                   | an Onsite Coordinator / Technical Lead role.                |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 4. Consular Interview Defense: Avoiding the 221(g) Administrative Trap

Consular officers aggressively test whether B1/L1 candidates understand their legal boundaries:

### Trap 1: The B1 "Hands-On Coding" Trap
- *Consul Question:* *"Will you be writing computer code or debugging software while in the United States on a B1 visa?"*
- **FATAL ANSWER:** ❌ *"Yes, I will be coding the payment gateway modules."* (Instant visa rejection under INA Section 214(b) - productive work is illegal on B1).
- **WINNING ANSWER:** ✔️ *"No, Officer. Productive coding and software development will be handled entirely by our offshore engineering team in India. My purpose is strictly to attend business meetings, gather system architecture specifications, and observe User Acceptance Testing (UAT) with the client."*

### Trap 2: The L1B "Specialized Knowledge" Question
- *Consul Question:* *"What specialized knowledge do you possess that an American engineer cannot perform?"*
- **WINNING ANSWER:** ✔️ *"I specialize in Infosys's proprietary banking platform framework, integrated with the client's internal legacy transaction ledger. I have spent over 30 months configuring its proprietary APIs, schema mapping, and automated reconciliation pipelines at our Bangalore development center."*
