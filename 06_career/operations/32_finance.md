## 2. Income Tax: Why You Pay ₹0 (Zero) Tax

Under the **New Tax Regime** (default tax regime in India):
- Total annual taxable income up to **₹7,00,000** receives a full tax rebate under **Section 87A**.
- Standard Deduction of **₹75,000** applies automatically to salaried professionals.
- Therefore, on a base CTC of **₹3.60 LPA**, your annual income tax liability is **strictly ₹0 (Zero)**.
- *Caution:* Do **NOT** purchase unnecessary life insurance or high-commission investment policies solely for tax-saving (Section 80C) purposes, as you already owe zero tax under the New Regime.

- **Universal Account Number (UAN):** Printed on your first monthly payslip.
  - Visit: `https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/`
  - Click **"Activate UAN"**, enter your UAN, Aadhaar, and mobile number.
  - Download your monthly passbook via the **UMANG** app or EPFO portal.

## 4. Gratuity: The 5-Year Continuous Service Rule

- **What is Gratuity?** A statutory retirement benefit mandated by the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.
- **Eligibility:** Payable only after completing **5 continuous years of service** at Infosys.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Gratuity} = \frac{15 \times \text{Last Drawn Basic Salary} \times \text{Number of Completed Years}}{26}$$
- *For an SE serving 5 years:* If your basic salary at year 5 is ₹25,000, your gratuity payout is:
  $$\frac{15 \times 25000 \times 5}{26} \approx \text{INR } 72,115 \text{ (Tax-Free)}$$

## 5. Form 16 & Filing Your First Income Tax Return (ITR-1)

Every financial year in June, Infosys generates your **Form 16**:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            FORM 16 & ITR-1 FILING GUIDE                         |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Step 1: Download Form 16 (Part A & Part B) from the Sparsh payroll portal.      |
|                                                                                 |
| Step 2: Log into the Income Tax e-Filing portal (`incometax.gov.in`) using PAN. |
|                                                                                 |
| Step 3: Select **ITR-1 (Sahaj)** for salaried individuals.                      |
|                                                                                 |
| Step 4: Salary details pre-fill automatically from Infosys's TDS filings.       |
|                                                                                 |
| Step 5: Verify Gross Income (~INR 3.6L) and confirm Net Tax Due is INR 0.      |
|                                                                                 |
| Step 6: Submit and e-verify via Aadhaar OTP within 30 days.                     |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
*(Filing your ITR annually is essential for building a clean financial track record, obtaining credit cards, and securing visas for overseas client projects).*
