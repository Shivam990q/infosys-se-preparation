## 1. Internal City Transfers (Relocating to Your Home State)

Many freshers are initially allocated to Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, or Chennai, but eventually want to transfer closer to their home city (e.g., Chandigarh, Indore, Nagpur, Bhubaneswar, or Trivandrum).

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                       INTERNAL LOCATION TRANSFER PROTOCOL                       |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Parameter         | Policy Guidelines                                           |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Minimum Tenure | Standard eligibility is **12 to 18 months of service** at   |
|                   | your base development center before initiating a transfer.  |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. Mechanism      | Initiated via **Compass (Internal Mobility Portal)** under  |
|                   | "Location Transfer Request" or by applying to open IJPs.    |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. Approvals      | Requires two sign-offs:                                     |
|                   | 1. Current Project Manager & Delivery Unit Head (release).  |
|                   | 2. Receiving Delivery Unit / DC Manager (acceptance).       |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. Compassionate  | Early transfers (<12 months) are approved on compassionate  |
|    Grounds        | medical grounds (e.g., severe parental illness or marriage).|
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 3. Campus Amenities & Work-Life Balance

Major development centers (Bengaluru Electronics City, Pune Phase 2, Hyderabad Pocharam, Chennai Sholinganallur) replicate the world-class infrastructure of Mysore:

1. **Fitness & Sports:**
   - Fully equipped gymnasiums with certified personal trainers.
   - Indoor wooden badminton courts, squash courts, and table tennis zones.
   - Outdoor floodlit cricket grounds and basketball/volleyball courts.
2. **Food & Retail:**
   - Multi-cuisine corporate food courts, Subway, Domino's, and Café Coffee Day.
   - On-campus supermarket and pharmacy.
3. **Annual Hackathons & Cultural Fests:**
   - **Infy Hackfest:** Annual competitive programming and AI hackathon with direct cash prizes and elevated role upgrades.
   - **Aarohan / Utsav:** Campus cultural festival featuring music, dance, and celebrity performances.

## 4. Grievance Redressal, POSH & Whistleblower Protection

Infosys enforces corporate governance standards that protect employees:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                       EMPLOYEE PROTECTION & COMPLIANCE                          |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Policy            | Protective Mechanism                                        |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. POSH (Sexual   | Governed by a dedicated Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).|
|    Harassment)    | Strict zero-tolerance; complaints investigated by independent|
|                   | women-led panels within 90 days. Confidentiality is legally |
|                   | protected.                                                  |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. Whistleblower  | Direct, encrypted, anonymous channel to report financial    |
|    Policy         | fraud, managerial corruption, or code-of-conduct breaches   |
|                   | directly to the Board of Directors with zero retaliation.   |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. HEAR Helpline  | Confidential 24/7 mental wellness and professional dispute  |
|                   | counseling service staffed by certified psychologists.      |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. Ombudsman      | Neutral arbitrator for resolving project allocation or      |
|                   | manager appraisal disputes without career repercussions.    |
+-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```
