# Registration, Application & ATS Resume Guide

**Role:** Systems Engineer (SE) | **Company:** Infosys Limited

---

## 1. Official Application Channels

Infosys conducts recruitment exclusively through authorized, verified platforms:
1. **On-Campus College Drives:** Conducted via a private college-specific registration portal (Infosys Launchpad or college placement portal like Superset) provided by your Training & Placement Officer (TPO).
2. **National Off-Campus Drives:** Posted exclusively on the official career portal: [**career.infosys.com**](https://career.infosys.com).

> [!CAUTION]
> **Recruitment Fraud Alert:**
> - Infosys **never charges any application fee, registration fee, or training deposit** at any stage.
> - Official emails originate **strictly from `@infosys.com` domains**. Never trust recruitment invites from Gmail, Yahoo, or generic domains.
> - Never apply via unverified Google Forms or third-party WhatsApp/Telegram survey links.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Application Walkthrough

1. **Access the Official Portal:** Follow the link provided in your college TPO circular or navigate to `career.infosys.com`.
2. **Personal & Academic Identity:**
   - Enter your full name **exactly as printed on your 10th standard marksheet / Aadhaar card**.
   - Use an active primary email address and permanent personal mobile number.
3. **Academic Mark Entry (Zero Rounding Rule):**
   - Enter your 10th, 12th/Diploma, and B.Tech aggregate percentages up to two decimal places.
   - **Do NOT round off.** (If your aggregate is 59.98%, do not enter 60.0%; automated document parsers will disqualify you during verification).
4. **Active Backlog Declaration:**
   - Declare active backlogs as **Zero (0)**. If you have an active backlog, you are not eligible.
   - If you had a previous backlog that was cleared, select "No active backlogs" and keep the cleared marksheets ready.
5. **Resume Upload:** Upload an ATS-compliant 1-page PDF resume.
6. **Confirmation & Reference ID:** Note down your Candidate Reference / Application ID for all future tracking.

## 3. ATS Resume Rules for Infosys Systems Engineer

Infosys utilizes automated Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to parse candidate profiles. Format your resume to maximize your match score:

### Resume Formatting Rules
- **Length:** Strictly **1 page**. Multi-page resumes are penalized for freshers.
- **File Format:** PDF format; file name format: `FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf`.
- **Layout:** Single-column layout. Avoid two-column layouts, tables, text boxes, icons, and progress bars (which break ATS parsing).
- **Font:** Clean, standard fonts (Calibri, Arial, Helvetica; 10–12pt body text, 14–16pt section headers).

### High-Impact Keywords to Include
Ensure these exact keywords appear naturally in your Skills and Projects sections:
- **Languages:** `Java`, `Python`, `C++`, `SQL`
- **Core Concepts:** `Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)`, `Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)`, `DBMS`, `Operating Systems`, `Computer Networks`
- **Database & Tools:** `MySQL` or `PostgreSQL`, `Git`, `GitHub`, `Postman`
- **Development Methodologies:** `Agile Methodologies`, `RESTful APIs`, `SDLC`

### How to Structure Academic Projects on Your Resume
Use this high-impact 3-bullet structure for every project:
- **Bullet 1 (Goal & Architecture):** Developed a [Project Name] using [Tech Stack: React, Spring Boot, MySQL] to [solve specific problem].
- **Bullet 2 (Technical Implementation):** Implemented [specific feature, e.g., JWT authentication, RESTful APIs, relational schema with 8 normalized tables].
- **Bullet 3 (Impact / Metric):** Handled [metric, e.g., optimized query execution time by 30%, tested across 50+ test cases].

## 4. Hardware & System Pre-Check Protocol

At least 24 to 48 hours before your scheduled online assessment, complete the official system pre-check:

1. **Direct Pre-Check URL:** Visit `https://rec-test.infosys.com/precheck`.
2. **Browser Validation:** Ensure you are using Google Chrome (Version 110+). Turn off pop-up blockers and extensions.
3. **Webcam & Mic Permissions:** When prompted, grant full camera and microphone permissions. Verify that your video feed displays with adequate front lighting.
4. **Bandwidth Test:** Ensure your internet connection achieves at least **2 Mbps upload and download speeds** with low latency.
5. **Clear Browser Cache:** Clear cookies and cache before launching the real assessment to prevent session dropouts.
