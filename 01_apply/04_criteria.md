# Eligibility Criteria & Filtration Rules

**Role:** Systems Engineer (SE) | **Company:** Infosys Limited

---

## 1. Academic Percentage / CGPA Benchmarks

Infosys uses automated programmatic screening. If an applicant fails to meet any one of these criteria, the application is discarded automatically without manual review.

| Qualification Tier | Minimum Required Marks | Scoring Scale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **10th Standard (SSC / Matriculation)** | **60.0%** or **6.0 CGPA** | Percentage or 10-point scale |
| **12th Standard / Diploma (HSC / Intermediate)** | **60.0%** or **6.0 CGPA** | Percentage or 10-point scale |
| **Graduation (B.E. / B.Tech - All Semesters Aggregate)** | **60.0%** or **6.0 CGPA** | Aggregate across all completed semesters to date |
| **Post-Graduation (M.Tech / MCA / M.Sc - If Applicable)**| **60.0%** or **6.0 CGPA** | Aggregate across all semesters |

> [!WARNING]
> **The Strict "No Rounding Off" Rule:**
> Infosys screening software enforces a zero-rounding rule. A score of **59.99%** or **5.99 CGPA** is strictly categorized as ineligible. Entering 60% when your actual transcript reflects 59.9% leads to immediate termination and blacklisting during Background Verification (BGV).

## 2. Eligible Degrees & Complete Branch Coverage

### Qualifying Degrees
- **B.E. / B.Tech:** All engineering degree holders.
- **M.E. / M.Tech:** All postgraduate engineering streams.
- **MCA:** Master of Computer Applications.
- **M.Sc:** Master of Science (Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics, Mathematics).

### Complete List of Eligible Engineering Branches
Unlike product companies that restrict drives to CSE/IT, **Infosys opens the Systems Engineer role to ALL engineering disciplines**:
- Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
- Information Technology (IT) & Information Science (ISE)
- Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
- Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE)
- Mechanical Engineering (ME)
- Civil Engineering (CE)
- Chemical Engineering
- Instrumentation & Control Engineering (ICE)
- Automobile & Aeronautical Engineering
- Biotechnology / Industrial / Production Engineering

*(Note: During Mysore training, students from non-CS branches receive identical foundation programming training as CS graduates).*

---

## 3. Backlog Policies (Live vs Cleared)

- **Active / Live Backlogs:** **Strictly Zero (0)** active backlogs permitted at the time of registration, online test, interview, and the final date of onboarding.
- **Dead / History of Backlogs:** Candidates who failed a subject in earlier semesters but have successfully cleared it before the recruitment drive are **fully eligible**, provided all official passing marksheets can be submitted.
- **Final Semester Risk:** If a selected candidate incurs a backlog in their final 8th semester, their offer is put on hold until the backlog is cleared in the supplementary exam.

---

## 4. Educational Gap Rules

- **Maximum Allowed Gap:** Up to **2 years (24 months)** cumulative gap across your entire academic timeline (e.g., gap between 10th and 12th, or drop year between 12th and B.Tech).
- **Gaps Exceeding 2 Years:** Require documented justification (medical emergencies, civil service preparation, family crises). Unexplained gaps will fail third-party Background Verification.
- **College Gap (Year-Back):** Candidates with a 1-year academic gap during their 4-year B.Tech (e.g., repeating a year) are eligible only if the total gap across their academic journey does not exceed 2 years.

---

## 5. Cooling-Off Period & Re-Application Restrictions

- **Mandatory Interval:** **6 to 9 Months**.
- If you have taken any Infosys online assessment or attended an Infosys technical/HR interview within the preceding 6–9 months, you are not eligible to apply.
- **Deduplication Engine:** Attempting to apply using an alternate email address or phone number will be detected by Infosys's deduplication system, which cross-checks your Government ID (Aadhaar / PAN Number), College Roll Number, and Full Name.

---

## 6. Citizenship & Documentation Requirements
- Must be a citizen of India (or hold valid permanent legal work authorization in India).
- Must possess a valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport).
- A valid **PAN Card** is mandatory for onboarding, payroll creation, and PF setup.
