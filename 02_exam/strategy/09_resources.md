## 2. Topic-by-Topic Video Master Series Breakdown

### A. Mathematical Ability (Quantitative Aptitude)
- **Time, Speed & Distance (Relative Speed & Trains):**
  - Watch: *CareerRide "Time Speed and Distance Shortcuts"* + *OnlineStudy4u Infosys Train Problems*.
  - Key focus: Train crossing man ($L/S$), trains crossing each other ($L_1 + L_2 / S_1 \pm S_2$), and boats & streams upstream/downstream formulas.
- **Time & Work (Pipes & Cisterns, Alternate Days):**
  - Watch: *Dear Sir "Time and Work Short Trick in Hindi"* (1-hour complete concept).
  - Key focus: LCM method for total work units, efficiency ratios, and negative work done by emptying pipes.
- **Permutations, Combinations & Probability:**
  - Watch: *Feel Free to Learn "Permutation and Combination Playlist"* (Episodes 1 to 6).
  - Key focus: Vowels together, circular permutations $(n-1)!$, conditional probability without replacement.
- **Data Interpretation (Tables, Bar Graphs, Pie Charts):**
  - Watch: *CampusMonk "Data Interpretation for Infosys"* (Focus on calculating percentage changes and ratios without full division).

---

### B. Logical Reasoning & Numerical Puzzles
- **Syllogisms (Venn Diagram Method):**
  - Watch: *Adda247 "Syllogism All, Some, No, Only a Few Rules"*.
  - Rule to master: Never assume reality; follow the exact circle intersection boundaries.
- **Data Sufficiency:**
  - Watch: *OnlineStudy4u "Data Sufficiency Tricks for Infosys"*.
  - Rule to master: Do not compute the exact numerical answer; only determine whether Statement (1) alone, Statement (2) alone, or both combined provide sufficient information.
- **Seating Arrangements (Circular & Linear):**
  - Watch: *Talent Battle "Infosys Seating Arrangement Short Tricks"*.
  - Rule to master: Always draw two parallel case diagrams if a condition has multiple possibilities, eliminating the invalid case as more clues are placed.
- **Numerical Puzzles (4 Puzzles in 10 Minutes):**
  - Watch: *FACE Prep "Infosys Numerical Puzzles Crash Course"*.
  - Focus archetypes: $3 \times 3$ grid logic (row/col sum), balance scale coin comparisons ($3^k \ge N$), burning ropes (45 minutes timing), and arithmetic operator insertion.

---

### C. Pseudocode & Algorithmic Tracing (The #1 Elimination Section)
- **Variable-Trace Tables:**
  - Watch: *Prime Coding "Infosys Pseudocode Trace Table Technique"*.
  - Practice: Creating a table on rough paper with columns `[Line, a, b, c, Condition, Output]`. Never trace loops purely in your head!
- **Bitwise Operations & Identities:**
  - Watch: *OnlineStudy4u "Infosys Pseudocode Bitwise AND OR XOR Left Shift"*.
  - Memorize:
    - $(a \ \& \ b) + (a \mid b) = a + b$
    - $a \oplus b = (a \mid b) - (a \ \& \ b)$
    - $a \ll k = a \times 2^k$
    - $a \gg k = \lfloor a / 2^k \rfloor$
- **Recursion Trees & Call Stacks:**
  - Watch: *Knowledge Gate "Recursion Tree Method and Output Prediction"*.
  - Practice: Tracing recursion calls on paper from top to bottom, recording return values as the call stack unwinds.

---

### D. Technical Interview & Core Computer Science Foundations
- **Object-Oriented Programming (OOPs):**
  - Watch: *Love Babbar CodeHelp "OOPs in C++ / Java (4 Pillars Explained with Real World Code)"*.
  - Be ready to code: Class vs Object, Encapsulation with private getters/setters, Inheritance types, Runtime Polymorphism (Virtual functions / Method Overriding), and Abstract classes vs Interfaces.
- **Database Management Systems (DBMS) & SQL:**
  - Watch: *Gate Smashers "DBMS Complete Playlist (Focus on Normalization & ACID)"*.
  - Watch: *Striver "Top 50 SQL Interview Queries"*.
  - Master queries: 2nd highest salary using `DENSE_RANK()`, `GROUP BY ... HAVING`, `INNER JOIN` vs `LEFT JOIN`, and `DELETE` vs `TRUNCATE` vs `DROP`.
- **Operating Systems & Computer Networks:**
  - Watch: *Gate Smashers "Operating Systems Playlist (Process vs Thread, Deadlocks, Virtual Memory)"*.
  - Watch: *Gate Smashers "Computer Networks (OSI 7 Layers, TCP 3-Way Handshake, DNS)"*.
