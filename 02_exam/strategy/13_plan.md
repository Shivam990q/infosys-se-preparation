## Week 1: Quantitative Foundations & Speed Math (Days 1–7)
*Daily Study Target: 2.5 to 3 Hours | Primary Platform: `indiabix.com` & OnlineStudy4u*

- **Day 1: Percentages & Fractions:**
  - Memorize fraction-to-percentage conversion tables ($\frac{1}{6} = 16.66\%, \frac{1}{7} = 14.28\%, \frac{1}{8} = 12.5\%, \frac{1}{9} = 11.11\%, \frac{1}{12} = 8.33\%$).
  - Practice 25 problems on successive percentage changes ($x + y + \frac{xy}{100}$). Check formulas in [06_syllabus.md](file:///c:/Users/Rose/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/c94c898d-a265-48e6-96c4-82b81795f2d9/02_exam/syllabus/06_syllabus.md).
- **Day 2: Profit, Loss & Discounts:**
  - Solve marked price, successive discounts, and fraudulent weight problems (Study Q3 in [19_pyqs.md](file:///c:/Users/Rose/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/c94c898d-a265-48e6-96c4-82b81795f2d9/02_exam/practice/19_pyqs.md)).
- **Day 3: Time, Speed & Distance (Part 1):**
  - Relative speed equations, trains crossing platforms and poles, average speed formulas ($\frac{2xy}{x+y}$). Study Q1 in [19_pyqs.md](file:///c:/Users/Rose/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/c94c898d-a265-48e6-96c4-82b81795f2d9/02_exam/practice/19_pyqs.md).
- **Day 4: Time, Speed & Distance (Part 2):**
  - Upstream/downstream boat problems ($u = \frac{D+U}{2}, v = \frac{D-U}{2}$) and circular track meeting points.
- **Day 5: Time & Work:**
  - Master the LCM efficiency method; solve alternate-day shift scheduling (Study Q2 in [19_pyqs.md](file:///c:/Users/Rose/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/c94c898d-a265-48e6-96c4-82b81795f2d9/02_exam/practice/19_pyqs.md)) and man-day formulas.
- **Day 6: Pipes & Cisterns:**
  - Practice inlet and drain leak problems (treating drain rate as negative efficiency).
- **Day 7: Permutations, Combinations & Probability:**
  - Word letter rearrangements with vowel constraints; committee selection; drawing cards/balls without replacement (Study Q4 & Q5 in [19_pyqs.md](file:///c:/Users/Rose/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/c94c898d-a265-48e6-96c4-82b81795f2d9/02_exam/practice/19_pyqs.md)).
