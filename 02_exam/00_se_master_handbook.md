### 4.1 High-Yield Topic Inventory & Core Frameworks
1. **Syllogisms (The Modern Rules):**
   * **"Only a few A are B":** Two statements hold simultaneously: (1) Some A are B, (2) Some A are NOT B. "All A are B" is strictly impossible.

   * **Complementary Pairs (Either-Or):** Some + No OR All + Some Not.
2. **Data Sufficiency:**
   * Never compute the answer! Only verify if a unique, single-valued answer exists. If a statement gives two possibilities (e.g. $x = 2$ or $x = 10$), it is **NOT SUFFICIENT**.

5. **Direction & Shadows:**

## 5. Section 3: Verbal Ability & English Grammar Deep Mastery

*Full Reference:* [42_verbal_mastery.md](file:///c:/Users/Rose/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/c94c898d-a265-48e6-96c4-82b81795f2d9/02_exam/syllabus/42_verbal_mastery.md)

### 5.1 The 12 Golden Grammar Rules
1. **Subject Isolation:** Intervening prepositional phrases (*along with, as well as, together with, in addition to*) do NOT alter the subject number.
2. **Correlative Proximity:** With *neither...nor / either...or*, verb agrees with the **nearest subject**.
3. **Indefinite Pronouns:** *Each, everyone, neither of, either of, one of* take strictly **singular verbs**.
4. **Dangling Modifiers:** Introductory participial phrase must modify the subject immediately after the comma.
5. **Parallelism:** Conjunctions (*not only...but also, both...and*) must link identical grammatical structures.
6. **Pronoun Cases:** Objective case (*between you and me*); comparison case (*he codes faster than I*).
7. **Tense Sequencing:** Past perfect (*had + V3*) is used only for the earlier of two past actions.
8. **Conditional Sentences:** Type 1 (*If + Present, will + V1*), Type 2 (*If + Past, would + V1*), Type 3 (*If + had + V3, would have + V3*).
9. **Redundancy:** Eradicate *revert back*, *return back*, *consensus of opinion*.
10. **Prepositions:** *Refrain from*, *adhere to*, *comply with*, *conducive to*.
11. **Para Jumbles:** Identify independent noun opener $\to$ pair with pronouns $\to$ sequence chronological bridge markers.
12. **Reading Comprehension Skimming:** 3-minute keyword skimming, author tone detection (objective, skeptical, didactic).

## 6. Section 4: Pseudocode Tracing & Bitwise Logic (#1 Elimination Vector)

*Full Reference:* [41_pseudocode_mastery.md](file:///c:/Users/Rose/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/c94c898d-a265-48e6-96c4-82b81795f2d9/02_exam/syllabus/41_pseudocode_mastery.md)

### 6.1 Operator Precedence Master Hierarchy
In Infosys pseudocode, syntax follows standard C/C++ precedence:
$$\text{Parentheses } () \;\to\; \text{Unary } (!, \sim, ++) \;\to\; \text{Multiplicative } (*, /, \%) \;\to\; \text{Additive } (+, -) \;\to\; \text{Shifts } (<<, >>) \;\to\; \text{Relational } (<, >) \;\to\; \text{Equality } (==, !=) \;\to\; \text{Bitwise } (\&, \wedge, |) \;\to\; \text{Logical } (\&\&, ||) \;\to\; \text{Ternary } (? :) \;\to\; \text{Assignment } (=)$$

> [!CAUTION]
> **The Critical Trap:** Bitwise operators (`&`, `^`, `|`) have **LOWER** precedence than comparison operators (`<`, `==`). `if (a & 1 == 0)` evaluates as `if (a & (1 == 0))`.

### 6.2 Key Bitwise Identities & Tracing Rules
* $x \oplus x = 0,\quad x \oplus 0 = x,\quad a \oplus b \oplus a = b$
* $(a \ \& \ b) + (a \mid b) = a + b$
* Unset rightmost bit: $x \ \& \ (x - 1)$
* Power of two: $(x > 0) \ \& \ ((x \ \& \ (x - 1)) == 0)$
* Two's complement: $-x = \sim x + 1$
* **The Variable-Trace Table:** Never trace loops mentally! On rough paper draw 4 columns: `[Step, Variable a, Variable b, Condition/Branch]`.

## 7. Section 5: Puzzle Solving & Cryptarithmetic Engine

*Full Reference:* [43_puzzles_cryptarithmetic.md](file:///c:/Users/Rose/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/c94c898d-a265-48e6-96c4-82b81795f2d9/02_exam/syllabus/43_puzzles_cryptarithmetic.md)

### 7.1 The 6 Invariants of Cryptarithmetic
1. Each letter is a unique single digit ($0–9$).
2. Leading letters can **NEVER be 0**.
3. **The Column Overflow Law:** Two digits can sum to at most $9 + 9 + 1 = 19$. Thus carry is at most 1. In $N$-digit $+$ $N$-digit $=$ $(N+1)$-digit, the **leading letter of sum is ALWAYS 1** ($M = 1$ in $SEND+MORE=MONEY$).
4. Parity laws: Even $+$ Even $=$ Even, Odd $+$ Odd $=$ Even, Even $+$ Odd $=$ Odd.
5. Multiplication unit digits: $6 \times \text{even ends in that same digit}$ ($6 \times 2 = 12, 6 \times 4 = 24, 6 \times 8 = 48$).

### 7.2 Classic Logic Puzzles
* **Balance Scale Coin Riddles:** Minimum weighings $k$ to detect 1 counterfeit coin among $N$ coins: $3^k \ge N$ (Divide into 3 equal piles).
* **Burning Ropes for 45 Mins:** Rope 1 light both ends (burns in 30 mins) + Rope 2 light one end. When Rope 1 finishes (at 30 mins), light the other end of Rope 2 (burns remaining 30 mins in 15 mins) $\implies 30 + 15 = 45$ mins.
* **River Crossing Torch Optimization:** The two slowest cross together to conserve torch burn time.
