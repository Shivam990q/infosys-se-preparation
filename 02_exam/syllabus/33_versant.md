## 3. Sample Questions & Winning Performance Scripts

### Part B: Repeat Sentences
- *Audio Prompt:* "The database backup process runs automatically at midnight."
- *Action:* Wait for the beep, then immediately repeat at normal conversational pace:  
  *"The database backup process runs automatically at midnight."*
- *Trap:* Do NOT rush or speak in a monotone robotic voice; maintain natural pauses at commas.

### Part D: Sentence Builds (Scrambled Audio)
- *Audio Prompt:* "in the conference room / the project lead / is waiting"
- *Your Spoken Output:* *"The project lead is waiting in the conference room."*

### Part F: Open Extempore Topics & 40-Second Formula
1. **Topic:** *"Do you prefer working from an office or working remotely from home?"*
2. **The 3-Step Speaking Template:**
   - **Step 1 (State Position - 10s):** *"I believe working from the office offers superior advantages, particularly for early-career software engineers."*
   - **Step 2 (Provide 2 Reasons - 20s):** *"Firstly, working on-campus enables spontaneous collaboration, faster debugging with senior architects, and immediate feedback during sprints. Secondly, dedicated office infrastructure eliminates home distractions and ensures strict enterprise network security."*
   - **Step 3 (Conclusion - 10s):** *"Therefore, while hybrid flexibility is helpful, physical office presence drives higher learning velocity."*

## 4. Hardware Setup & Avoiding AI Scoring Zero

The AI speech recognizer will penalize or fail you if audio artifacts occur:

1. **Wired Headset Mandate:** Use a high-quality USB or 3.5mm wired headset with an attached boom microphone. **NEVER use laptop built-in microphones** (they pick up room echoes and keyboard clicks).
2. **Microphone Positioning:** Place the mic **two fingers away from your mouth**, slightly below your lower lip. Placing it directly in front of your mouth causes "plosive air pops" (p/b sounds) that distort waveform analysis.
3. **Volume Check:** Run the voice calibration test. Ensure your input waveform stays in the green zone (avoid red clipping).
4. **Zero Fillers Rule:** If you forget a word, pause silently for 1 second instead of saying *"ummm..."* or *"aaaah..."*. The AI interprets long fillers as lack of fluency.
