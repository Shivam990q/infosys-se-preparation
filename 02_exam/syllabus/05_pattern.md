**Platform:** Infosys Assessment Engine (`rec-test.infosys.com`)  
**Format:** Timed, Adaptive/Qualifying, Multi-Sectional Online Test

## 2. Elevated Role Assessment (DSE & SP Track)
Candidates who perform exceptionally well on the SE test (or who are invited via college coding hackathons / HackWithInfy) take the **Advanced Coding Assessment**:
- **Duration:** 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
- **Format:** 3 Coding Problems:
  - Problem 1: Easy (Arrays / Strings / HashMaps)
  - Problem 2: Medium (Greedy Algorithms / Two Pointers / Trees)
  - Problem 3: Hard (Dynamic Programming / Graph Theory)
- **Role Assignment:** Solving 1–1.5 problems qualifies you for **DSE (₹6.25 LPA)**; solving 2.5–3 problems qualifies you for **Specialist Programmer (₹9.50 LPA)**.

---

## 3. The 4 Non-Negotiable Platform Mechanics

1. **Zero Backward Navigation:** Once you submit or skip a question, you **cannot return**. There is no "mark for review" or section summary screen.
2. **Independent Sectional Countdown Timers:** Each section operates on its own dedicated timer. If you complete Verbal Ability 5 minutes early, that time **is discarded** and does not roll over to Mathematical Ability.
3. **No Negative Marking:** There is no deduction for wrong answers. Always make a calculated guess before a section's timer expires.
4. **Mandatory Sectional Cutoffs:** You must pass **every single section independently**. Getting 100% in Math, Logic, and Verbal will still result in rejection if you score 0/4 in Puzzle Solving.

### Critical Violations that Cause Auto-Disqualification
- **Headphones / Earphones:** Wearing any wired or wireless earphones, AirPods, or gaming headsets triggers an immediate violation flag.
- **Eye Movement & Face Tracking:** The AI model tracks your gaze. Looking down at your lap (e.g., at a mobile phone) or frequently turning your head triggers an alert.
- **Secondary Persons / Ambient Speech:** Any background whispering or another face appearing in the frame results in test invalidation.
- **Window Switching / Alt-Tab:** The secure browser detects focus shifts. Switching tabs, opening Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, or taking screenshots will terminate your test on the 2nd attempt.

## 5. Technical Specifications & Pre-Check
- **Hardware:** Desktop or Laptop with Intel Core i3 (or AMD Ryzen equivalent), minimum 4GB RAM, working HD webcam, and functioning microphone. Mobile devices and tablets are strictly prohibited.
- **Browser:** Google Chrome (Version 110 or higher). Disable all ad-blockers, translate plugins, and pop-up blockers.
- **Internet:** Stable connection with minimum 2 Mbps upload/download speed. Avoid mobile hotspots where incoming calls can disconnect your data stream.
- **Pre-Check Link:** Execute the mandatory system test at `https://rec-test.infosys.com/precheck` at least 24 hours prior to test day.
