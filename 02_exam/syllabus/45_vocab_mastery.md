## 1. Reading Comprehension (RC) Master Architecture & The Tone Formula

### A. The SUV Rule for Infosys RC Speed
- **S — Speed:** Skim passage in 2 to 2.5 minutes using first/last sentence skimming.
- **U — Understanding:** Grasp central theme, structural shifts, and author attitude before touching questions.
- **V — Vocabulary:** Contextually deduce unfamiliar adjectives from surrounding contrast/continuation markers.

### B. The 6 Core Cognitive Powers
1. **Analytical Ability:** Deconstructing complex claims into premise and conclusion.
2. **Logical Ability:** Detecting fallacies, unstated assumptions, and syllogistic bounds.
3. **Reasoning Ability:** Evaluating argument strength and identifying weak counter-evidence.
4. **Inference Power:** Extracting what MUST be true strictly from the text, without assuming outside reality.
5. **Reading Speed:** Expanding eye fixation span to read chunks of 4–5 words simultaneously.
6. **Vocabulary Power:** Instantly recognizing authorial bias through loaded adjectives and adverbs.

### C. The 10 Critical Reading Steps
`Meaning → Paragraphs → Major/Minor → Conclusions → Missing Info → Structure → Assumptions/Perspective → Analysis → Strengths/Weaknesses → Alternatives`

### D. The TONE Formula & Mnemonic
$$\mathbf{TONE} = \text{Author's Mood} + \text{Voice} + \text{Attitude} \longrightarrow \text{Expressed through Adjectives, Adverbs & Figures of Speech}$$
**Exam Shortcut:** `TONE → MVA → AAF` *(Mood + Voice + Attitude → Adjectives + Adverbs + Figures of Speech)*
