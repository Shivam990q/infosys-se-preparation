### Question 1 [Source: `photo_833@28-10-2024_22-28-23.jpg`, `photo_835@28-10-2024_22-28-23.jpg`]
**Question:** Distribution of people affected by COVID-19 across districts of Uttar Pradesh.

**Tabular Data (Clean Redraw):**
| District Name | Total Population Sample | Confirmed Affected Cases | Percentage Affected |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Saharanpur** | 5,800 | 1,000 | $17.24\%$ |
| **Ghaziabad** | 6,750 | 1,000 | $14.81\%$ |
| **Varanasi** | 8,010 | 1,000 | $12.48\%$ |
| **Lucknow** | 6,490 | 1,000 | $15.40\%$ |

**Question:** What percentage of people in Saharanpur district were affected?
* **(A)** $17.24\%$
* **(B)** $14.81\%$
* **(C)** $12.48\%$
* **(D)** $15.40\%$

**Correct Answer:** **(A) 17.24%**  
**Solution:**
$$\text{Percentage} = \frac{\text{Affected}}{\text{Total}} \times 100 = \frac{1000}{5800} \times 100 = \frac{100}{5.8} = \mathbf{17.24\%}$$
