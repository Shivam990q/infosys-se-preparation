### Question 1 [Source: `photo_1041@28-10-2024_22-34-35.jpg`]
**Passage:**
> *"Appearing for an IELTS test can be daunting, so don't forget to put these tips into action and practice strategically to hit your target band score and get into your dream college. Focused preparation and daily structured practice eliminate uncertainty and stress."*

**Question:** Read the above given passage carefully and answer the following question based on it:
*What should a student do to ensure that the IELTS exam experience is less daunting?*

**Answer Options:**
* **(A)** By making a strategic plan and putting proven practice tips into action
* **(B)** By ranting about it
* **(C)** By taking help from seniors only
* **(D)** By purchasing more resources

**Correct Answer:** **(A) By making a strategic plan and putting proven practice tips into action**  
**Explanation:**
* The text explicitly commands the candidate to *"put these tips into action and practice to hit the score"*. This directly correlates with proactive, structured strategy over panic or passive resource accumulation.
