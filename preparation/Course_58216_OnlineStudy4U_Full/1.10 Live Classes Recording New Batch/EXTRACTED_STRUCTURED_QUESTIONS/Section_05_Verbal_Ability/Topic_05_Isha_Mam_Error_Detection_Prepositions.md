Error Detection exercises on Preposition, Conjunction and Determiners 
1. Identify the sentence with the correct preposition: 
            a) She is interested on learning new languages. 
          b) She is interested in learning new languages. 
           c) She is interested at learning new languages. 
           d) She is interested for learning new languages. 
2. Choose the sentence that uses the correct preposition: 
            a) He is good in playing the guitar. 
           b) He is good at playing the guitar. 
           c) He is good on playing the guitar. 
           d) He is good for playing the guitar. 
3. Identify the sentence with the correct preposition: 
            a) The book is on the table. 
           b) The book is in the table. 
           c) The book is at the table. 
           d) The book is by the table. 
4. Find the sentence with the incorrect preposition: 
            a) We will meet at 5 PM. 
           b) We will meet in 5 PM. 
           c) We will meet on 5 PM. 
           d) Both b and c are incorrect. 
5. Which sentence correctly uses the preposition? 
            a) She is afraid from spiders. 
           b) She is afraid of spiders. 
           c) She is afraid about spiders. 
           d) She is afraid for spiders. 
6. Identify the sentence with the correct preposition: 
            a) He arrived to the airport early. 
           b) He arrived at the airport early. 
           c) He arrived in the airport early. 
           d) He arrived for the airport early.
