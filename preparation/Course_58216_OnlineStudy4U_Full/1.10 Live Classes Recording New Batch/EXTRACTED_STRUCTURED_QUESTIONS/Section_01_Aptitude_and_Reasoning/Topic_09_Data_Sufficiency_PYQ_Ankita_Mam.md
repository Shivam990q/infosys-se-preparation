### The Foundational Infosys Data Sufficiency Decision Matrix

In the Infosys System Engineer Reasoning Section, Data Sufficiency problems test whether Statement (1) and Statement (2) supply mathematically and logically sufficient information to guarantee a unique, unambiguous answer.

#### Standard 5-Option Conventions:
- **A.** Statement (1) ALONE is sufficient to answer the question, but statement (2) alone is not sufficient.
- **B.** Statement (2) ALONE is sufficient to answer the question, but statement (1) alone is not sufficient.
- **C.** BOTH statements (1) and (2) TOGETHER are sufficient to answer the question, but NEITHER statement alone is sufficient.
- **D.** EACH statement ALONE is sufficient to answer the question.
- **E.** Statements (1) and (2) TOGETHER are NOT sufficient to answer the question, and additional data is required.

### Problem 1: Integer Properties & Parity
**Category:** Number Systems & Algebraic Parity
**Question Statement:** Is x an even integer?
**Statement 1:** 3x + 5 is an odd integer.
**Statement 2:** x^2 + 2x is an even integer.
**Correct Answer:** **Option A** (Statement 1 alone is sufficient)
**Explanation:** 3x + 5 = odd => 3x = odd - 5 = even. Since 3 is odd, x must be an even integer. Sufficient.

### Problem 2: Linear Simultaneous Pricing Systems
**Category:** Commercial Arithmetic & Algebra
**Question Statement:** What is the price of 1 notebook and 1 pen?
**Statement 1:** Total price of 3 notebooks and 3 pens is Rs. 150.
**Statement 2:** Total price of 2 notebooks and 5 pens is Rs. 140.
**Correct Answer:** **Option A** (Statement 1 alone is sufficient)
**Explanation:** 3N + 3P = 150 => N + P = 50. Directly sufficient without requiring individual prices of N or P.

### Problem 3: Age Relations & Linear Equations
**Category:** Word Problems on Ages
**Question Statement:** What is the present age of Rohit?
**Statement 1:** Rohit is 5 years older than his brother Aman.
**Statement 2:** Five years ago, ratio of Rohit age to Aman age was 4 : 3.
**Correct Answer:** **Option C** (Both statements together are needed)
**Explanation:** R - A = 5 and (R-5)/(A-5) = 4/3. Combining yields R = 25 years, A = 20 years.

### Problem 4: Speed, Distance & Relative Motion
**Category:** Time, Speed & Distance
**Question Statement:** How long will Train A take to completely cross a stationary platform of length 200 meters?
**Statement 1:** Train A crosses a standing pole in 12 seconds.
**Statement 2:** Train A crosses Train B running in opposite direction at 54 km/h in 10 seconds.
**Correct Answer:** **Option E** (Statements 1 and 2 together are NOT sufficient)
**Explanation:** Statement 1 gives LA = 12*SA. Statement 2 introduces unknown length of Train B (LB). 3 variables with 2 equations cannot determine SA or time.

### Problem 5: Blood Relations & Tree Sufficiency
**Category:** Logical Reasoning - Family Tree
**Question Statement:** How is P related to T?
**Statement 1:** P is the brother of Q; Q is the mother of R; R is the sister of T.
**Statement 2:** T is the son of S; S is the husband of Q.
**Correct Answer:** **Option A** (Statement 1 alone is sufficient)
**Explanation:** From (1), Q is mother of T, and P is brother of Q. P is definitely maternal uncle of T.

### Problem 6: Time and Work Combined Systems
**Category:** Arithmetic - Time & Work
**Question Statement:** In how many days can C alone finish the work?
**Statement 1:** A and B together can finish in 12 days; B and C together can finish in 15 days.
**Statement 2:** A, B, and C together can finish in 8 days.
**Correct Answer:** **Option C** (Both statements together are sufficient)
**Explanation:** A+B = 1/12 and A+B+C = 1/8 => C = 1/8 - 1/12 = 1/24. C finishes alone in 24 days.
