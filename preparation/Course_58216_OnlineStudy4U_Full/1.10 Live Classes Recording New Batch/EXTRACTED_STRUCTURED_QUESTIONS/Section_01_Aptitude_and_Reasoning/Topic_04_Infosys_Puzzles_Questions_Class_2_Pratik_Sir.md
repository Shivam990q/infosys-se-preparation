### Problem 1 (Wrong Number Series)
**Slide Reference:** `images/apt_pratik_c2_page_05.png`  
**Problem Statement:**  
Find the wrong number in the sequence:  
$$26, 52, 91, 117, 141, 195$$

**Options:**  
- **A.** 91  
- **B.** 117  
- **C.** 141  
- **D.** 195  

**Correct Answer:** **Option C** (`141`)

**Detailed Step-by-Step Derivation:**  
1. Check divisibility by 13:  
   - $26 = 13 \times 2$  
   - $52 = 13 \times 4$  
   - $91 = 13 \times 7$  
   - $117 = 13 \times 9$  
   - $195 = 13 \times 15$  
   Every number in the sequence is an exact multiple of 13 except **141** ($141 = 13 \times 10 + 11$).  
2. If following $+26, +39, +26, +26$, $117 + 26 = 143$.  
3. Therefore, the wrong number is **141**.

### Problem 2 (Salary Percentage Chain)
**Slide Reference:** `images/apt_pratik_c2_page_07.png`  
**Problem Statement:**  
Vikram's salary is 20% more than that of Ajay and Ajay's salary is 10% less than that of Chetan. By what percentage is Vikram's salary more than that of Chetan?

**Options:**  
- **A.** 8%  
- **B.** 10%  
- **C.** 12%  
- **D.** 15%  

**Correct Answer:** **Option A** (`8%`)

**Detailed Step-by-Step Derivation:**  
1. Let Chetan's salary $C = 100$.
2. Ajay's salary is 10% less than Chetan: $A = 0.90 \times 100 = 90$.
3. Vikram's salary is 20% more than Ajay:  
   $$V = 1.20 \times 90 = 108$$
4. Vikram's salary compared to Chetan:  
   $$\text{Percentage More} = \frac{108 - 100}{100} \times 100\% = \mathbf{8\%}$$

### Problem 3 (Work & Efficiency)
**Slide Reference:** `images/apt_pratik_c2_page_08.png`  
**Problem Statement:**  
A and B can finish a job together in 12 days while B alone can finish it in 30 days. In how many days can A finish the job alone?

**Options:**  
- **A.** 20 days  
- **B.** 18 days  
- **C.** 24 days  
- **D.** 15 days  

**Correct Answer:** **Option A** (`20 days`)

**Detailed Step-by-Step Derivation:**  
$$\text{Total Work} = \text{LCM}(12, 30) = 60 \text{ units}$$
$$E_{A+B} = \frac{60}{12} = 5 \text{ units/day}, \quad E_B = \frac{60}{30} = 2 \text{ units/day}$$
$$E_A = 5 - 2 = 3 \text{ units/day}$$
$$T_A = \frac{60}{3} = \mathbf{20 \text{ days}}$$
