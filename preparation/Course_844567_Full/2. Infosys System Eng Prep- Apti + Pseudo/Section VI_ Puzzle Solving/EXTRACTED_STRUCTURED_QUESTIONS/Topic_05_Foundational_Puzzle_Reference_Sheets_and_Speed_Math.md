### 4. Primary Mathematical Grid Formulations for Infosys

1. **Quadratic Sum Grid**: $A^2 + B^2 - 2 = C$ (Row 4: $5^2 + 8^2 - 2 = 87$)
2. **Linear Product Grid**: $(R_1 \times R_3) + R_2 = R_4$ (Col 3: $(21 \times 3) + 9 = 72$)
3. **Alphabet Product Grid**: $\text{Pos}(Letter) = \text{Top} \times \text{Bottom}$ ($3 \times 6 = 18 = R$)
4. **Radial Mean Grid**: $\text{Center} = \frac{\text{Left} + \text{Right}}{2}$ ($ (9 + 3)/2 = 6 $)
5. **Constant Quadrant Sum**: $T + L + R + B = 22$ (Remaining missing term = 9)
