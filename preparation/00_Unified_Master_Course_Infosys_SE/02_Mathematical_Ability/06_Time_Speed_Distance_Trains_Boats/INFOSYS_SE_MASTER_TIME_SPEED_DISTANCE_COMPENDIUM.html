QUESTION 3[OnlineStudy4U] INFOSYS TEST SERIES-2

(A)60
(B)65
(C)72
(D)80
✔ Correct Answer: (C)
Speed = Distance/Time = 200/10 = 20 m/s = 20×18/5 = 72 km/hr. Correction → Answer is 72 km/hr.
