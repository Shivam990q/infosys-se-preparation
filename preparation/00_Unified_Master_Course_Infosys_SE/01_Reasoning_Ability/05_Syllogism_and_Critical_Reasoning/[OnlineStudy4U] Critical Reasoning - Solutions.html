Question 1

Statement: Should school uniforms be made compulsory for students?

Arguments:

I. Yes, uniforms create equality among students and remove discrimination based on clothing.

II. No, students should have the freedom to wear what they want.

  • (A)

    Only Argument I is strong

    CORRECT
  • (B)

    Only Argument II is strong

  • (C)

    Both I and II are strong

  • (D)

    Neither I nor II is strong

Correct Answer: A
Explanation:

Argument I is strong as it supports discipline and equality. Argument II is weak as it focuses only on personal freedom without considering the benefits of uniforms.

Question 2

Statements:

1. The electricity demand has sharply increased, leading to frequent power outages.

2. The city has experienced its hottest summer in the last 50 years.

  • (A)

    Statement 1 is the cause, and Statement 2 is its effect.

  • (B)

    Statement 2 is the cause, and Statement 1 is its effect.

    CORRECT
  • (C)

    Both statements are independent causes.

  • (D)

    Both statements are effects of a common cause.

Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

The extreme heat (Statement 2) caused an increase in electricity usage (Statement 1), leading to power outages.

Question 12

More than one billion years’ worth of rocks have gone missing from the geologic record of Grand Canyon.

  • (A)

    Earth has transitioned from an older setting to the modern one during these one billion years.

  • (B)

    The western half of the Grand Canyon has gone through a very different geologic contortion compared to the eastern half.

  • (C)

    Series of Faulting events in that region possibly tore up the earth around the Canyon causing rocks and sediments to wash away in the ocean.

    CORRECT
  • (D)

    None of the mentioned options.

Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

In a new study, researchers led by Peak identify a likely cause for the missing rocks: the breakup of Earth's ancient supercontinent Rodina approximately 700 million years ago. The upheaval was so violent it likely washed rocks and sediment into the ocean — one billion years’ worth, in the case of the Grand Canyon.

Question 15

Directions: In the question below, a statement and assumptions numbered I and II are given. An

assumption is something supposed or taken for granted. You have to consider the following assumption

and decide which of the assumption is implicit in the statement.

Statement: “Tomorrow morning the daily review meeting will happen at 1 pm instead of 11 am” said the boss.

Assumptions:

I. That day boss will arrive at the office only at 1 pm.

II. Some of the employees will not be available at 11 am.

  • (A)

    Only Assumption I is implicit

  • (B)

    Only Assumption II is implicit

  • (C)

    Both I and II are implicit

  • (D)

    Neither I nor II is implicit

    CORRECT
Correct Answer: D
Explanation:

The statement does not mention when the boss will arrive (I is not implicit).

The reason for rescheduling is unclear, so employee unavailability is not necessarily assumed (II is not implicit).

Question 16

Statement:

Mother scolding the child said, “If you continue doing naughty things, I will slap you.”

Assumptions:

I. All children are naughty.

II. With this warning, child may stop doing mischievous acts.

  • (A)

    Only Assumption I is implicit

  • (B)

    Only Assumption II is implicit

    CORRECT
  • (C)

    Both I and II are implicit

  • (D)

    Neither I nor II is implicit

Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

Assumption I: "All children are naughty" is not implicit because the statement talks about one specific child, not all children in general.

Assumption II: "With this warning, the child may stop doing mischievous acts" is implicit because the mother’s warning suggests that she believes it will have an effect on the child's behavior.
