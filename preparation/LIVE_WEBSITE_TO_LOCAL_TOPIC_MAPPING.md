## 🚫 Next Step Policy: No Random PDF Generation

Aapka kehna 100% sahi hai:
1. **Random Compendiums Stopped:** Hum ab koi bhi naya bulky PDF bina aapke review ke nahi banayenge taaki printing me paper waste na ho aur random study se confusion na ho.
2. **Review First:** Aap pehle upar diye gaye topic folders aur mock test hub ko aaram se inspect karein.
3. **Targeted Selection:** Jis specific topic ka aapko crisp, high-yield, printable summary sheet chahiye hoga, aap batayenge aur hum sirf usi topic ki targeted compact PDF banayenge.
