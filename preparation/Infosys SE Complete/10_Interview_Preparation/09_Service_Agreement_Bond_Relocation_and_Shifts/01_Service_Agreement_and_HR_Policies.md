# 📝 Infosys Service Agreement, Relocation Policy & Shift Rotations

---

## 1. Key Operational Policies
- **Service Agreement (Bond):** Typically 12 months (1 year) post-training. Penalty of INR 1,00,000 applies if breached without completion of required term.
- **Relocation Policy:** Systems Engineers must be open to relocation across any Infosys Development Centre (DC) in India (Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mysore, Bhubaneswar, Chandigarh, Indore, Jaipur, Mangaluru, Thiruvananthapuram).
- **Shift Rotations:** Projects supporting global clients (US, Europe, APAC) operate on 24/7 rotational shifts. Answering *"Yes, I am comfortable working in rotational shifts and flexible environments"* is mandatory for clearance.
