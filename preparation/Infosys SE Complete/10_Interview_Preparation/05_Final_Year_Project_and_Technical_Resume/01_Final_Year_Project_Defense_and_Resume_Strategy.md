# 🎓 Final Year Engineering Project Defense & Resume Strategy

> **Crucial Tip:** The interview panelist will almost certainly spend 10 to 15 minutes asking detailed questions about your academic project.

---

## 1. The 5-Step Project Explanation Framework
1. **The Problem Statement (Why was it built?):** State the real-world problem or inefficiency your project addresses.
2. **Architecture & Technology Stack:** List languages, frameworks, databases, and APIs used (e.g. React frontend, Spring Boot backend, PostgreSQL database).
3. **Your Specific Individual Role:** Clearly state what modules you personally designed, coded, and tested.
4. **Key Technical Challenge Faced & Resolution:** Explain a specific bug, latency bottleneck, or integration issue and how you solved it.
5. **Future Enhancements & Impact:** Where can this project be taken in an enterprise production environment?
