# 🏢 Infosys Corporate Knowledge, History & C-LIFE Core Values

---

## 1. Key Facts About Infosys
- **Founded:** July 2, 1981, in Pune, India by 7 engineers (N.R. Narayana Murthy, Nandan Nilekani, S. Gopalakrishnan, S.D. Shibulal, K. Dinesh, N.S. Raghavan, and Ashok Arora).
- **Headquarters:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
- **CEO & MD:** Salil Parekh.
- **Global Presence:** Operates across 50+ countries with 300,000+ employees.
- **Key Modern Offerings:** Infosys Topaz (Generative AI platform), Infosys Cobalt (Cloud suite), Infosys Springboard.

## 2. The C-LIFE Core Values
- **C:** Customer Delight
- **L:** Leadership by Example
- **I:** Integrity and Transparency
- **F:** Fairness
