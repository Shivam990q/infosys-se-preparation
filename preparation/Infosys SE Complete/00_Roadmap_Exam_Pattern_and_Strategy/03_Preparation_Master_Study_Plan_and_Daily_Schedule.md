# 📅 30-Day Master Study Plan & Daily Hour-by-Hour Schedule

> **Target:** 100% Mastery of Infosys Systems Engineer (SE) Recruitment Assessment  
> **Study Commitment:** 4–6 Hours Daily  
> **Design Principle:** Interleaved Learning (Never study only one subject in a single day)

---


---

## 2. Week-by-Week Detailed Roadmap

### Week 1: Foundational Arithmetic & Syntactic Grammar
- **Day 01–02:** Number Systems, Divisibility Rules, LCM/HCF, Unit Digits. Subject-Verb Agreement (39 Rules of Concord).
- **Day 03–04:** Percentages, Profit, Loss & Discount. Prepositions and Phonetic Articles.
- **Day 05–06:** Ratio & Proportion, Partnerships, Averages & Mixtures. Sentence Correction & Parallelism.
- **Day 07:** Weekly Review, Sectional Test 1 (Quant + Grammar), Error Log Review.

### Week 2: Logical Deduction & Computational Execution
- **Day 08–09:** Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Coding-Decoding. Spotting Errors (50 Traps).
- **Day 10–11:** Linear & Circular Seating Arrangements, Double Row Facing. Bitwise Operators (`&`, `|`, `^`, `<<`, `>>`).
- **Day 12–13:** Syllogisms (Possibility & Either-Or Cases), Data Sufficiency (Reasoning). Loop Boundary & Dry-Run Trace Tables.
- **Day 14:** Weekly Review, Sectional Test 2 (Reasoning + Pseudo Code).

### Week 3: Puzzles, Enterprise Reading & Higher Math
- **Day 15–16:** Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns, Relative Speed & Trains. Technical Reading Comprehension Passages.
- **Day 17–18:** Permutations, Combinations & Probability. Cloze Tests & Sentence Fillers.
- **Day 19–20:** 2-Mark & 3-Mark Grid Puzzles, Missing Polygon Numbers. Para-Jumbles (Cohesive flow).
- **Day 21:** Weekly Review, Sectional Test 3 (Puzzles + Verbal + WAT Essay).

### Week 4: Full-Length Exam Simulations & Velocity Conditioning
- **Day 22:** Full-Length Mock Exam 1 (CampusMonk Day 01) + In-depth Error Analysis.
- **Day 23:** Full-Length Mock Exam 2 (OnlineStudy4U Mock 1) + Remedial Weak Spot Practice.
- **Day 24:** Full-Length Mock Exam 3 (College ITM Paper 1) + Pseudocode Recursion Drills.
- **Day 25:** Full-Length Mock Exam 4 (Topmate 2nd Oct Slot 1) + Speed Reading Training.
- **Day 26:** Full-Length Mock Exam 5 (OnlineStudy4U Mock 2) + WAT Essay Timed Formulation.
- **Day 27:** Full-Length Mock Exam 6 (College ITM Paper 2) + Puzzle Pattern Recognition.
- **Day 28:** Full-Length Mock Exam 7 (CampusMonk Day 02) + Final Formula Sheet Rehearsal.
- **Day 29:** Light Review: High-Frequency Vocabulary Sheet, SVA Cheatsheet, Bitwise XOR Axioms.
- **Day 30:** Rest, Mental Conditioning, System Setup & Document Readiness.
