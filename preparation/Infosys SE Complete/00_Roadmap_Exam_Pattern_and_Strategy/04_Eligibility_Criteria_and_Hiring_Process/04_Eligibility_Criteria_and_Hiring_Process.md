# 📋 Infosys Recruitment Eligibility Criteria, Selection Stages & Service Agreement

> **Profile:** Systems Engineer (SE)  
> **Compensation Package:** INR 3.6 LPA – 4.0 LPA (Core SE)  
> **Progression Paths:** Systems Engineer → Senior Systems Engineer → Technology Analyst / Lead Consultant

---

## 1. Academic Eligibility Criteria

- **Target Degrees:** B.E. / B.Tech / M.E. / M.Tech in any engineering discipline (CS, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, etc.), or MCA / M.Sc (Computer Science / IT).
- **Academic Cut-off Aggregate:**
  - 10th Standard: **Minimum 60%** (or equivalent CGPA).
  - 12th / Diploma: **Minimum 60%** (or equivalent CGPA).
  - Graduation (B.Tech / Degree): **Minimum 60% or 6.0 CGPA** on a 10-point scale across all semesters.
- **Active Backlogs:** **Zero (0) Active Backlogs** permitted at the time of appearing for the recruitment process or onboarding.
- **Education Gap:** Maximum permitted academic gap of **up to 12 months (1 year)** between educational milestones, provided valid justification is furnished.
- **Prior Assessment Cooling-off Period:** Minimum **3 months (90 days)** cooling period required if previously appeared for any Infosys selection test.

---

## 2. Multi-Stage Selection Pipeline

```
[Stage 1: Online Proctored Recruitment Assessment (60 Qs + 1 Essay | 120 Mins)]
                                    ↓
[Stage 2: Technical & HR Unified Interview (25–35 Mins via Infosys Video Platform)]
                                    ↓
[Stage 3: Letter of Intent (LOI) & Background Verification (BGV)]
                                    ↓
[Stage 4: Offer Letter Release & Mysore Induction Reporting]
```

### Stage 1: Online Proctored Assessment
- AI-monitored camera, microphone, full-screen lockdown.
- Face tracking, gaze deviation detection, tab-switch prevention.
- Strict sectional timers and independent section cut-offs.

### Stage 2: Technical & HR Virtual Interview
- Conducted by Senior Systems Engineers or Technology Leads.
- Focus Areas: Final year engineering project, core OOPs principles (Java, C++, or Python), basic SQL queries, DBMS normalization, operating systems fundamentals, and behavioral HR scenarios.

### Stage 3 & 4: Offer & Onboarding
- Verification of marks sheets, provisional degree certificates, Aadhaar/Passport.
- Digital offer letter issuance and scheduling for Infosys Global Education Centre (GEC), Mysore.
