# 🏰 Infosys Mysore Training (GEC) — Complete Curriculum, Life & Exit Exams

> **Location:** Infosys Global Education Centre (GEC), Mysore, Karnataka, India  
> **Campus Scale:** 337 Acres — The largest corporate university in the world  
> **Training Duration:** 3 to 5 Months (Structured into Generic & Stream-Specific Training)

---

## 1. Mysore Training Architecture Overview

Training at Infosys Mysore is legendary for its rigor, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and strict academic standards. It is divided into two major phases:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        INFOSYS MYSORE TRAINING PHASES                             |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Foundation / Generic Training (6–8 Weeks): Core Programming, OOPs, DBMS, Web   |
|                                    ↓                                              |
| 2. Focus Area / Stream Training (8–10 Weeks): Specialized Tech Track Deployment   |
|    (Java Full Stack, Python/Cloud, .NET, Data Engineering, Salesforce, SAP)      |
|                                    ↓                                              |
| 3. Final Comprehensive Capstone Project & Branch Allocation                       |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 2. Generic Training Curriculum (FA1 & FA2 Exams)

### Generic Phase 1 (Core Foundations):
- **Problem Solving & Algorithms:** Flowcharts, pseudocode, time complexity, array manipulation.
- **Core Programming Language:** Python or Java fundamentals (Data types, control structures, collections, exception handling).
- **Object-Oriented Programming (OOPs):** Encapsulation, Inheritance, Polymorphism, Abstraction, Interface design.
- **Relational Databases & SQL:** RDBMS concepts, DDL/DML queries, Joins, Subqueries, Normalization (1NF to 3NF).

### Focus Area 1 (FA1) Examination:
- Hands-on coding exam + Objective multiple choice questions.
- **Passing Threshold:** Minimum **65% aggregate**. Candidates scoring <65% must appear for re-tests.

---

## 3. Stream Training & Capstone Project

Candidates are allocated to specialized technological streams based on project demand:
- **Stream Options:**
  - Java Enterprise Full Stack (Spring Boot, Angular/React, Microservices).
  - Python Cloud & Data Analytics (Django/FastAPI, AWS/Azure, PostgreSQL).
  - Modern Cloud Infrastructure & DevOps (Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines).
  - Enterprise ERP / CRM Platforms (SAP ABAP, Salesforce, ServiceNow).

### Focus Area 2 (FA2) & Comprehensive Assessment:
- Enterprise capstone project development in teams simulating real-world client deliverables.
- Final Comprehensive Examination evaluating end-to-end full stack architecture.

## 4. Life at Mysore Campus & Facilities

- **Accommodation:** Multiplex-style single occupancy rooms (ECC - Employee Care Centre) with automated laundromats, daily housekeeping, and study desks.
- **Recreation:** Olympic-size swimming pool, bowling alley, badminton/tennis courts, cricket ground, 8-screen multiplex cinema.
- **Food Courts:** 5 international food courts catering to cuisines from all regions of India and global options.
