## 1. Executive Assessment Structure Overview

The Infosys Systems Engineer (SE) selection examination is an intensive **120-minute, 60-question + 1 descriptive prompt** test engineered to evaluate analytical velocity, computational logic, syntactic precision, and enterprise writing capabilities. 

### Critical Operational Rules:
1. **Strict Non-Transferable Sectional Timers:** Each section has an independent, non-cumulative countdown timer. Candidates cannot borrow time from one section to use in another.
2. **One-Way Navigation Across Sections:** Once a section's timer expires or the candidate manually submits the section, the test permanently advances. Candidates cannot return to previous sections.
3. **Internal Section Navigation:** Within an active section, candidates can navigate forward and backward between questions, mark questions for review, and revise answers until the section timer lapses.
4. **Mandatory Independent Sectional Cut-offs:** Infosys strictly enforces an independent cut-off threshold (typically 65%–70%) for *every single section*. Scoring 100% in Quantitative Aptitude will not compensate for failing Pseudocode or Puzzle Solving.

## 3. Exhaustive Domain-by-Domain Syllabus Breakdown

### Domain 1: Reasoning Ability (15 Questions | 25 Minutes)
- **Data Sufficiency (2-Statement Logic):** Evaluating if Statement (1) alone, Statement (2) alone, both combined, or neither can uniquely resolve the problem.
- **Seating Arrangement:** Linear arrangements (single row, parallel double rows with opposite facing), Circular arrangements (inward, outward, mixed orientations), Square and rectangular perimeter placement.
- **Syllogisms & Logical Inferences:** 2-statement and 3-statement categorical propositions, 'Some', 'All', 'No', 'Some Not', along with advanced 'Either-Or' conditions and possibility cases.
- **Coding-Decoding & Pattern Series:** Direct letter shifting, reverse alphabet value mapping, symbol-digit matrix ciphering, and progressive arithmetic shifts.
- **Blood Relations & Family Trees:** Direct relations, indirect pointer statements (*"Pointing to a photograph..."*), coded blood relations (`P + Q means P is father of Q`).
- **Direction & Distance Tracking:** Multi-step angular turns (45°, 90°, 135°, 180° clockwise and counter-clockwise), Pythagorean distance calculation, shadow orientations at sunrise/sunset.
- **Critical & Deductive Reasoning:** Statement & Assumptions, Statement & Conclusions, Course of Action, Cause & Effect, Argument strength evaluation.
- **Visual & Non-Verbal Reasoning:** Dice manipulation (opposite faces), paper folding & cutting, figure completion, mirror & water reflections.

### Domain 2: Technical Ability - Quantitative Aptitude (10 Questions | 35 Minutes)
- **Commercial Mathematics:** Percentages, Profit, Loss & Discount, Marked Price, Successive Discounts, Simple Interest and Compound Interest (annual, semi-annual compounding).
- **Time, Work & Efficiency:** Unitary work method, negative efficiency (Pipes & Cisterns with leakages), alternate day working, wage distribution based on work share.
- **Speed, Time & Distance:** Relative speed, linear races, train crossing stationary objects vs moving platforms, upstream and downstream river navigation.
- **Ratios, Proportions & Partnerships:** Direct and inverse proportionality, compound ratios, investment capital-time weighted partnership shares, ages and mixtures/alligations.
- **Higher Arithmetic & Combinatorics:** Permutations & Combinations (distinct arrangements, grouping, circular permutations), Probability (independent events, conditional probability, ball selection, dice and cards).
- **Number Systems & Divisibility:** Prime factorization, LCM & HCF word applications, divisibility rules (7, 11, 13, 17, 19), unit digits of large powers, remainder theorems.
- **Mensuration & Geometry (2D & 3D):** Perimeter, area, volume, total surface area of cylinders, cones, spheres, cubes, cuboids, and frustums.
- **Data Interpretation (DI):** Tabular datasets, bar charts, multi-variable line graphs, pie charts (percentage-based and degree-based), and mixed graph comparative analysis.

### Domain 3: Verbal Ability (20 Questions | 20 Minutes)
- **Reading Comprehension (RC):** 2 to 3 technical, scientific, or socioeconomic passages (300–500 words each). Tests contextual vocabulary, factual recall, author's primary purpose, tone identification, and inferential deduction.
- **Sentence Correction & Grammar:** Modifiers (dangling and misplaced modifiers), parallel structure in lists, subject-verb agreement with intervening parenthetical clauses, tense consistency.
- **Spotting Errors:** Four-fragment sentence decomposition `[A] / [B] / [C] / [D]` targeting prepositional misuse, pronoun case ambiguity, and double comparatives.
- **Para Jumbles & Cohesive Flow:** Re-sequencing 4 to 5 scrambled sentences `[P, Q, R, S]` to form a logically sound paragraph using pronoun-antecedent links, chronology markers, and contrasting conjunctions.
- **Sentence Completion & Cloze Tests:** Single and double blank sentences testing precise diction, collocation knowledge, and phrasal verb comprehension.
- **Lexical Competency:** Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, Phrasal Verbs, and One Word Substitution.

### Domain 4: Pseudo Code (5 Questions | 10 Minutes)
- **Bitwise Logic & Masking:** Bitwise AND (`&`), OR (`|`), XOR (`^`), NOT (`~`), Left Shift (`<<`), and Right Shift (`>>`).
- **Loop Boundary Conditions:** Loop termination conditions, off-by-one errors, nested `for` and `while` loop execution counts.
- **Recursion & Call Stacks:** Base case termination, recursive call branching, accumulator tracking, and stack unwind values.
- **Operator Precedence & Associativity:** Unary, arithmetic, relational, logical, and ternary assignment operator order.
- **Scope & Parameter Passing:** Pass-by-value vs pass-by-reference simulation, global vs local variable shadowing, static variable retention.

### Domain 5: Puzzle Solving (4 Questions | 10 Minutes)
- **Numerical Grid & Matrix Puzzles:** 3×3 and 4×4 number grids where missing elements are determined by row/column sum, product, square-difference, or modular relationships.
- **2-Mark & 3-Mark High-Weightage Puzzles:** Non-trivial puzzles carrying higher marks (2 marks for standard, 3 marks for advanced grid puzzles).
- **Geometric & Polygon Puzzles:** Triangles, circles, and star polygons where vertex numbers operate algebraically to produce central values.
- **Cryptarithmetic & Cross-Number Puzzles:** Letter-to-digit substitution puzzles where unique digits satisfy basic arithmetic operations.

### Domain 6: English Grammar (5 Questions | 10 Minutes)
- **Inverted Third Conditionals:** Structures beginning with `Had + subject + past participle, ... would have + past participle`.
- **Subjunctive Mood:** Verbs following mandative expressions (`demand that he be...`, `insist that she go...`).
- **Phonetic Article Usage:** Rules governing silent letters (`an heir`, `an honest man`, `a university`, `a European nation`).
- **Dependent Prepositions & Collocations:** Verbs with mandatory prepositions (`averse to`, `insist on`, `prohibit from`, `comply with`).

### Domain 7: Written Ability Test (WAT) (1 Prompt | 10–20 Minutes)
- **Descriptive Essay Formulation:** 200–300 words typed in a real-time text area. Evaluated on paragraph cohesion, logical argumentation, technical vocabulary, sentence variety, and absence of spelling/grammatical errors.
