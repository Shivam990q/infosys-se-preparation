# ⏱️ Section-Wise Time Management, Strategic Pacing & Cut-off Thresholds

> **Core Rule:** Sectional cut-offs are independent and mandatory. If you fail any single section, your entire assessment is disqualified regardless of overall score.

---

## 2. Minute-by-Minute Time Management Playbook

### Section 1: Reasoning Ability (15 Questions in 25 Minutes)
- **Minutes 00:00 – 10:00 (The Quick Strike - 7 Qs):** Attack standalone items first: Direction sense, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, and simple Syllogisms. Spend no more than 60–80 seconds per question.
- **Minutes 10:00 – 20:00 (The Deep Deduction - 6 Qs):** Tackle Data Sufficiency and linear/circular seating arrangements. Draw neat scratch diagrams.
- **Minutes 20:00 – 25:00 (Buffer & Review - 2 Qs):** Attempt remaining marked questions and ensure zero unattempted items (no negative marking).

### Section 2: Technical Ability / Quantitative Aptitude (10 Questions in 35 Minutes)
- **Allocation:** Generous **3.5 minutes (210 seconds) per question**.
- **Strategy:**
  1. *First Pass (0–15 Mins):* Solve 5 direct arithmetic problems (Percentages, Profit & Loss, Ratio, Time & Work).
  2. *Second Pass (15–30 Mins):* Solve Data Interpretation sets and combinatorics/probability questions.
  3. *Final 5 Mins:* Cross-check arithmetic steps and verify unit conversions (e.g. km/h to m/s).

### Section 3: Verbal Ability (20 Questions in 20 Minutes)
- **Allocation:** Strict **60 seconds per question**.
- **Strategy:**
  - *Minutes 0–8:* Solve Spotting Errors, Sentence Completion, Para Jumbles, and Vocabulary (10–12 questions).
  - *Minutes 8–18:* Read the 2 Reading Comprehension passages and answer the questions. Read questions first to anchor target facts in the passage.
  - *Minutes 18–20:* Ensure all options are selected.

### Section 4: Pseudo Code (5 Questions in 10 Minutes)
- **Allocation:** **2.0 minutes per question**.
- **Golden Rule:** Never guess variable states mentally. Maintain a structured **Dry-Run Trace Table** on scratch paper tracking iteration indices, conditional branches, and bitwise updates.

### Section 5: Puzzle Solving (4 Questions in 10 Minutes)
- **Allocation:** **2.5 minutes per puzzle**.
- **Strategy:**
  - Scan all 4 puzzles in the first 30 seconds.
  - Prioritize standard 3×3 grid puzzles where row/column patterns are recognizable.
  - If a puzzle involves non-linear algebraic exponents, test extreme values (0, 1, 2) or examine parity (odd/even properties).

### Section 6: English Grammar (5 Questions in 10 Minutes)
- **Allocation:** **2.0 minutes per question**.
- **Strategy:** High precision required. Check:
  1. What is the true grammatical subject? (Ignore prepositional phrases like *as well as, along with*).
  2. Is the conditional clause inverted?
  3. Is the preposition idiomatically fixed to the preceding verb?

### Section 7: Written Ability Test (1 Prompt in 15 Minutes)
- **Minutes 0–2:** Brainstorm 3 distinct arguments and thesis statement.
- **Minutes 2–11:** Type a cohesive 4-paragraph essay:
  - Paragraph 1: Introduction & thesis definition (40–50 words).
  - Paragraph 2: Core supporting arguments with real-world technical/societal examples (80–100 words).
  - Paragraph 3: Counter-perspective or challenge & practical remediation (70–80 words).
  - Paragraph 4: Strong concluding summary (40–50 words).
- **Minutes 11–15:** Proofread for typographical mistakes, subject-verb agreement, and sentence flow.
