## Problem 031 (ID: `SEC02_QUANT_TRAIN_Q01`)
- **Source Module**: `2. Numerical ability`
- **Source File**: `TSD 01 TRAIN.pdf`
- **Topic Domain**: **Time, Speed and Distance: Train Crossing Platform**

### Question Statement
A train 150 meters long is running at a speed of 54 km/hr. How much time will it take to pass a platform 200 meters long?

### Options
- A. 23.33 seconds
- B. 25 seconds
- C. 20 seconds
- D. 30 seconds

### Correct Answer
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Verified Answer Key**: `A. 23.33 seconds`

### Step-by-Step Mathematical Proof & Arithmetic Solution
1. Convert speed to m/s: Speed = 54 * (5 / 18) = 15 m/s.
2. Total distance to cross platform = Length of Train + Length of Platform = 150 + 200 = 350 meters.
3. Time = Distance / Speed = 350 / 15 = 70 / 3 = 23.33 seconds.

## Problem 032 (ID: `SEC02_QUANT_BOAT_Q01`)
- **Source Module**: `2. Numerical ability`
- **Source File**: `bosts and stream One Shot.pdf`
- **Topic Domain**: **Boats and Streams: Upstream & Downstream**

### Question Statement
A man can row 18 km/hr in still water. It takes him thrice as long to row up as to row down the river. Find the rate of stream.

### Options
- A. 9 km/hr
- B. 6 km/hr
- C. 4.5 km/hr
- D. 12 km/hr

### Correct Answer
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Verified Answer Key**: `A. 9 km/hr`

### Step-by-Step Mathematical Proof & Arithmetic Solution
Let speed of stream = y km/hr. Speed in still water x = 18 km/hr.
Downstream speed = 18 + y.
Upstream speed = 18 - y.
Given: Time upstream = 3 * Time downstream.
Since distance is constant, Speed downstream = 3 * Speed upstream.
18 + y = 3 * (18 - y)
18 + y = 54 - 3y
4y = 36 -> y = 9 km/hr.
