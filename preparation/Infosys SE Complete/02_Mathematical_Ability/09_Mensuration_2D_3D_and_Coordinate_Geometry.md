## Problem 015 (ID: `SEC02_QUANT_MENS_Q01`)
- **Source Module**: `2. Numerical ability`
- **Source File**: `mensuration3D.pdf`
- **Topic Domain**: **Mensuration 2D & 3D: Cylinder Melting to Spheres**

### Question Statement
A solid metallic cylinder of base radius 3 cm and height 5 cm is melted to make spherical balls of radius 1 cm. How many such balls can be made?

### Options
- A. 33
- B. 34
- C. 30
- D. 35

### Correct Answer
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Verified Answer Key**: `A. 33`

### Step-by-Step Mathematical Proof & Arithmetic Solution
Volume of cylinder = π * r^2 * h = π * 3^2 * 5 = 45π cm^3.
Volume of one spherical ball = (4/3) * π * R^3 = (4/3) * π * 1^3 = (4/3)π cm^3.
Number of balls = Volume of Cylinder / Volume of one ball
Number = 45π / [(4/3)π] = 45 * 3 / 4 = 135 / 4 = 33.75.
Since only complete solid balls can be formed, exactly 33 complete spherical balls can be made.

## Problem 016 (ID: `SEC02_QUANT_QUAD_Q01`)
- **Source Module**: `2. Numerical ability`
- **Source File**: `01 Quadratic Equations - New.pdf`
- **Topic Domain**: **Quadratic Equations: Root Comparison**

### Question Statement
Compare the roots of the two equations:
I. x^2 - 11x + 30 = 0
II. y^2 - 15y + 56 = 0

### Options
- A. x < y
- B. x > y
- C. x ≤ y
- D. x ≥ y
- E. Relationship cannot be established

### Correct Answer
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Verified Answer Key**: `A. x < y`

### Step-by-Step Mathematical Proof & Arithmetic Solution
1. Solve equation I: x^2 - 11x + 30 = 0.
Product = 30, Sum = 11 -> Roots are x = 5, 6.
2. Solve equation II: y^2 - 15y + 56 = 0.
Product = 56, Sum = 15 -> Roots are y = 7, 8.
3. Compare each x with each y:
- x=5 < y=7, 8
- x=6 < y=7, 8
In all cases, x < y.
Therefore, x < y.
