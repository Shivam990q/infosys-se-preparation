## Pedagogical Strategy & Error Categorization
In the Infosys System Engineer exam, Error Detection and Sentence Correction represent the highest-yield testing categories. Questions generally appear in three structural formats:
1. **Four-Part Sentence Split**: A sentence divided into segments `(A) / (B) / (C) / (D)`, with `(E) No error` or selecting the faulty part.
2. **Underlined Phrase Substitution**: An underlined or bracketed clause where option (A) is identical to original, and (B), (C), (D) offer syntactic alternatives.
3. **Sentence Improvement / Complete Sentence Choice**: Four distinct full sentences where exactly one is grammatically flawless.

Below is the exhaustive, forensic compendium of every authentic error detection and sentence correction question extracted across all curriculum repositories.
