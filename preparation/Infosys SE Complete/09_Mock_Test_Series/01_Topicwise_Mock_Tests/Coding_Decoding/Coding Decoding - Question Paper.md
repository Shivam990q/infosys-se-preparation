### Question 1 [Marks: +{'positive': 4, 'negative': 1, 'negativeUnattempted': 0}]

In
a certain code language, 'OM' is coded as 56 and 'GOT' is coded as
126. How will 'CARD' be coded in that language?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 104
- **(B)** 144
- **(C)** 140
- **(D)** 142

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Verified Answer Key:** ✅ **(B) 144**

**Step-by-Step Explanation & Derivation:**
**Explanation / Solution:**
OM = (15+13)*2 = 56
GOT = (7+15+20)*3 = 126
CARD = (3+1+18+14) * 4 = 144
