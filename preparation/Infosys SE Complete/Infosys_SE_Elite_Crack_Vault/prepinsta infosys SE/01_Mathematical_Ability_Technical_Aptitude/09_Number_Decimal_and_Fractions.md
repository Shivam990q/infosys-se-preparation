## 📋 Section 1: Rules to Keep in Mind
These rules and fundamental principles govern all Infosys questions on this topic:

- BODMAS / PEMDAS operator priority rule.
- Converting recurring decimals to vulgar fractions: 0.bar(a) = a/9; 0.bar(ab) = ab/99; 0.a bar(b) = (ab - a)/90.
- Comparing fractions: Cross-multiply numerators and denominators to determine relative size.

## 🚀 Section 3: Tricks & Shortcuts
Key mathematical formulas, mental-math shortcuts, and speed axioms:

- Decimal multiplication: Multiply ignoring decimals, then place decimal point equal to total sum of decimal places.
- If numerator and denominator have same difference, fraction with larger numbers is greater.

### Question 6 (PrepInsta quiz-1 - Q6)

**Options:**
- **(A)** `5/8`
- **(B)** `8/5`
- **(C)** `6/7`
- **(D)** `7/6`

**Correct Answer:** **(D)** `7/6`

**PrepInsta Detailed Explanation & Mathematical Derivation:**
```text
Let the required fraction be p/q.


Given that, sum of the numerator and denominator of a fraction is 13;


i.e., p + q = 13 ...(1)


Adding 3 to numerator and 9 to the denominator, we have,


(P+3)/(q+9) = 2/3


3(P+3) = 2(q+9)


3p+9 = 2q+18


3p-2q = 9 ...(2)


Multiply eqn (1)by 2, 2p + 2q = 26...(3)


Adding (2) and (3),


5p = 35


p = 7.


Putting p value in (1), we get q = 13 - p = 13 - 7 = 6.


Hence the required fraction is p/q = 7/6
```

### Question 10 (PrepInsta quiz-1 - Q10)
**Question Stem:**
If the sum and difference of two numbers are 15 and 3 respectively. Then the difference of their square is?

**Options:**
- **(A)** `276`
- **(B)** `216`
- **(C)** `176`
- **(D)** `188`

**Correct Answer:** **(B)** `216`

**PrepInsta Detailed Explanation & Mathematical Derivation:**
```text
Let the nos be “a” and “b”.


According to question,


a+b=15


and, a-b=3


 a^{2} - b^{2} = (a+b)(a-b) 


 a^{2} - b^{2} = (15+3)(15-3) 


 a^{2} - b^{2} = (18)(12) 


 a^{2} - b^{2} = 216
```
