## 📋 Section 1: Rules to Keep in Mind
These rules and fundamental principles govern all Infosys questions on this topic:

- If present age is x, age n years ago was (x - n), and age n years hence will be (x + n).

- If age ratio at two different time points is given, set up a single-variable linear equation.

**PrepInsta Detailed Explanation & Mathematical Derivation:**

```text
Hence present age of daughter is 10 years and present age of father is 40 years.


So after 5 + 5 = 10 years, daughter age would be 20 years and father’s age would be 50 years.


Hence father would be 50/20 = 2.5 times of daughter’s age
```

### Question 3 (PrepInsta quiz-1 - Q3)
**Question Stem:**
Ten years ago, the age of mother was three times the age of her son. After ten years, mother’s age will be twice that of his son. Find the ratio of their present ages

**Options:**
- **(A)** `11 : 7`
- **(B)** `9 : 5`
- **(C)** `7 : 4`
- **(D)** `7 : 3`


**Correct Answer:** **(D)** `7 : 3`

**PrepInsta Detailed Explanation & Mathematical Derivation:**
```text
10 years ago, age of mother was three times the age of her son. Say, the age of son was x and mother\'s age was 3x.


At present: Mother age is (3x + 10) and son’s age is (x + 10)


After ten years: Mother age will be (3x + 10) +10 and son’s age will be (x + 10) + 10. Given that, mother’s age is twice that of son after ten years.


(3x + 10) +10 = 2 [(x + 10) + 10]


(3x + 20) = 2[x + 20]


Solving the equation, we get x = 20


(3x + 10): (x + 10) = 70: 30 = 7: 3
```

```text
Let us assume x years ago


 At present: Sharad is 60 years and Santosh is 80 years


 x years ago: Sharad’s age = (60 – x) and Santosh’s age = (80 – x)


Ratio of their ages x years ago was 4 : 6


So, (60-x)/(80-x) = 4/6


=&gt;6(60 – x) = 4(80 – x)


=&gt;360 – 6x = 320 – 4x


=&gt;x = 20
```

**Question Stem:**
5 years ago, sister’s age was 5 times the age of her brother and the sum of present ages of sister and brother is 34 years. What will be the age of her brother after 6 years?

**Options:**
- **(A)** `12 years`
- **(B)** `13.5 years`
- **(C)** `15 years`
- **(D)** `20 years`


**Correct Answer:** **(C)** `15 years`

**PrepInsta Detailed Explanation & Mathematical Derivation:**
```text
Let, x=Present age of brother


5 years ago sister’s age was 5 times the age of her brother.


Therefore,


(34 – x) – 5 = 5 (x – 5)


=&gt;34 – x – 5 = 5x – 25


=&gt;5x + x = 34 – 5 +25


=&gt;6x = 54


=&gt;x = 9


Future age (after 6 yrs) = (x + 6) = (9 + 6) = 15 years
```
