## 📋 Section 1: Rules to Keep in Mind
These rules and fundamental principles govern all Infosys questions on this topic:

- Difference Series: Constant difference, AP difference, Difference of differences (2nd order).
- Multiplication/Division Series: *n, /n, *(n+1), *(n-1).
- Powers Series: n^2, n^3, n^2 +/- 1, n^3 +/- 1, n^3 +/- n.
- Alternate Series: Two interleaved series operating on odd and even indices independently.
- Fibonacci Series: Each term is the sum of preceding terms.

2+ 3 = 5
