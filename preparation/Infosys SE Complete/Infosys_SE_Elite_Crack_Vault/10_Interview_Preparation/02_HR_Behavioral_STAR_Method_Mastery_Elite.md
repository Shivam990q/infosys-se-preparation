# 🤝 Behavioral HR Questions & The STAR Method Framework

> **The STAR Technique:**
> - **S (Situation):** Set the scene and provide context.
> - **T (Task):** Describe your responsibility or challenge.
> - **A (Action):** Explain the exact steps YOU took.
> - **R (Result):** Share the measurable outcome and what you learned.
