### Question 1

Find
the missing element in the given series:
48
? 36 90 315 1417.5

**Options:**

- (A) 16
- (B) 30
- (C) 45
- ✅ **(D) 24** *(Correct Answer)*

**Correct Answer:** (D)



**Explanation / Solution:**
#### Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation:
1. **Analyze Sequence Terms:** $48, ?, 36, 90, 315, 1417.5$
2. **Examine Multiplication Ratios of Known Adjacent Terms:**
   - 90 / 36 = 2.5
   - 315 / 90 = 3.5
   - 1417.5 / 315 = 4.5
3. **Deduce Uniform Common Ratio Step:**
   - The multipliers increase by +1.0 at each progressive step (0.5, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5).
4. **Calculate Missing Second Term:**
   - First term * 0.5 = 48 * 0.5 = 24.
   - Verify next step: 24 * 1.5 = 36 (Matches!).
- **Final Result:** Missing element is **24** (Option D).

### Question 2

Find
the wrong term in the given series:
10
12 18 42 162 ?

**Options:**

- ✅ **(A) 882** *(Correct Answer)*
- (B) 828
- (C) 456
- (D) 768

**Correct Answer:** (A)



**Explanation / Solution:**
#### Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation:
1. **Analyze Sequence Terms:** $10, 12, 18, 42, 162, ?$
2. **Compute Successive Differences:**
   - 12 - 10 = 2
   - 18 - 12 = 6 = 2 * 3
   - 42 - 18 = 24 = 6 * 4
   - 162 - 42 = 120 = 24 * 5
3. **Determine Pattern of Differences:**
   - The differences are factorial progressions: Delta_n = Delta_{n-1} * (n+1).
   - Next difference Delta_5 = 120 * 6 = 720.
4. **Calculate Next Term:**
   - 162 + 720 = 882.
- **Final Result:** Next term is **882** (Option A).

### Question 7

Find
the missing element in the given series:
379
390 412 445 ? 544

**Options:**

- (A) 462
- (B) 511
- ✅ **(C) 489** *(Correct Answer)*
- (D) 538

**Correct Answer:** (C)



**Explanation / Solution:**
#### Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation:
1. **Analyze Sequence Terms:** $379, 390, 412, 445, ?, 544$
2. **Compute First Differences:**
   - 390 - 379 = 11 = 11 * 1
   - 412 - 390 = 22 = 11 * 2
   - 445 - 412 = 33 = 11 * 3
3. **Progressive Multiples of 11:**
   - Next difference must be 11 * 4 = 44.
   - Missing term = 445 + 44 = 489.
4. **Verify Boundary with 544:**
   - 544 - 489 = 55 = 11 * 5 (Matches!).
- **Final Result:** Missing element is **489** (Option C).
