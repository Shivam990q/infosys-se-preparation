Question 1

How many students are in the class?

Statements:

I. Ramesh is ranked 12th from the top.

II. Suresh is ranked 8th from the bottom.

III. There are 5 students between Ramesh and Suresh.

  • (A)

    Only I and II together are sufficient

  • (B)

    Only II and III together are sufficient

  • (C)

    Only I and III together are sufficient

  • (D)

    All I, II, and III together are sufficient

    CORRECT
  • (E)

    Even all statements together are not sufficient

Correct Answer: D
Explanation:

From Statement I, Ramesh is 12th from the top.

From Statement III, there are 5 students between Ramesh and Suresh, meaning Suresh’s rank from the top is 18th.

From Statement II, Suresh is 8th from the bottom.

Since Suresh is 18th from the top and 8th from the bottom, the total number of students = 18 + 8 - 1 = 25.

All three statements together are required to determine the total number of students.

Question 2

Question: Who among P, Q, T, V and M is exactly in the middle when they are arranged in ascending order of their heights ?

Statements:

I. V is taller than Q but shorter than M.

II. T is taller than Q and M but shorter than P.

  • (A)

    I alone is sufficient while II alone is not sufficient

  • (B)

    II alone is sufficient while I alone is not sufficient

  • (C)

    Either I or II is sufficient

  • (D)

    Neither I nor II is sufficient

  • (E)

    Both I and II are sufficient

    CORRECT
Correct Answer: E
Explanation:

From I, we have: M > V > Q.

From II, we have: T > Q, T > M, P > T.

Combining the above two, we have: P>T>M>V>Q i.e. Q

Clearly, M is in the middle.

Statements:

I. DEAR is written as $#@? in that code.

II. TREAT is written as %?#@% in that code.

III. TEAR is written as %#@? in that code.

  • (A)

    Only I and II together are sufficient

  • (B)

    Only II and III together are sufficient

  • (C)

    Only I and III together are sufficient

  • (D)

    All I, II, and III together are sufficient

    CORRECT
  • (E)

    Even all statements together are not sufficient

Correct Answer: D
Explanation:

Observing I, II and III, we find that similar letters have similar code symbols at the corresponding places in the code. So, this is direct-coding.

Question 6

I. In a row having 15 people, A is sitting second to the right of B who is sitting at twelfth position from the right end of row.

    II alone is sufficient while I alone is not sufficient

  • (C)

    Either I or II is sufficient

    CORRECT
  • (D)

    Neither I nor II is sufficient

  • (E)

    Both I and II are sufficient

Question 10

What is the present age of Sunita?

Statements:

I. Five years ago, Sunita's age was half of what it will be in 10 years.

II. The difference between Sunita's present age and twice her age five years ago is 10 years.

  • (A)

    I alone is sufficient while II alone is not sufficient

  • (B)

    II alone is sufficient while I alone is not sufficient

  • (C)

    Either I or II is sufficient

    CORRECT
  • (D)

    Neither I nor II is sufficient

  • (E)

    Both I and II are sufficient

Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

From statement I: Let Sunita’s present age be x.

Five years ago, her age was (x - 5).

In 10 years, her age will be (x + 10).

Given: (x - 5) = ½ (x + 10)

Solving for x, we get x = 20.

From statement II: Let Sunita’s age be x.

Twice her age five years ago = 2(x - 5).

Given: x - 2(x - 5) = 10

Solving for x, we get x = 20.

Question 13

Can a water tank of 2500 litres capacity be filled by two inlet pipes A and B together in less than 8 hours?

I. If pipe A is opened alone then it takes 12 hours to fill the tank.

II. Pipe B can fill 1 litres of water per minute.

  • (A)

    I alone is sufficient while II alone is not sufficient

  • (B)

    II alone is sufficient while I alone is not sufficient

  • (C)

    Either I or II is sufficient

  • (D)

    Neither I nor II is sufficient

  • (E)

    Both I and II are sufficient

    CORRECT
Correct Answer: E
Explanation:

From the statement I, the efficiency of pipe A per hour = 2500/12 = 625/3 litres per hour

From the statement II, the efficiency of pipe B = 1 × 60 = 60 litres per hour

Pipe A and B together will fill in 1 hour = 625/3 + 60 = 805/3 litres per hour

The time it will take to fill 2500 litres of water = more than 8 hours, therefore answer will be no

Therefore, the data in both the statements I and II together is necessary to answer the question

Question 16

What is the sum of the angles of a triangle?

I. One of the angles of the triangle is 60 degrees.

II. The triangle is an equilateral triangle.

  • (A)

    I alone is sufficient while II alone is not sufficient

  • (B)

    II alone is sufficient while I alone is not sufficient

    CORRECT
  • (C)

    Either I or II is sufficient

  • (D)

    Neither I nor II is sufficient

  • (E)

    Both I and II are sufficient

Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

Step 1: Checking Statement I

It only tells us that one of the angles is 60 degrees, but it does not give information about the sum of all angles.

However, the sum of the angles in any triangle is always 180 degrees, which is a known fact.

Statement I alone is NOT sufficient, as it does not help determine whether the general rule of 180° holds.

Step 2: Checking Statement II

An equilateral triangle has all three angles equal to 60 degrees.

Since we know it's a triangle, its angles must sum to 180 degrees by definition.

Statement II alone is sufficient, as it confirms the sum of angles.
