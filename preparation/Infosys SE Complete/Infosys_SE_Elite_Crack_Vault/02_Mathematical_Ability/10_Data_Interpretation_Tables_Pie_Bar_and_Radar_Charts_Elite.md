Question 1: If 5500 copies are published and the transportation cost on them amounts to Rs. 82,500, then what should be the selling price of the book so that the publisher can earn a profit of 25%?

### Options
- A. Rs. 187.50
- B. Rs. 191.50
- C. Rs. 175.00
- D. Rs. 180.00

### Correct Answer
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Verified Answer Key**: `A. Rs. 187.50`

Given Transportation cost for 5500 copies = Rs. 82,500.

Total Cost of 5500 copies = 82,500 * (100 / 10) = Rs. 8,25,000.
Cost price (CP) per book = 8,25,000 / 5500 = Rs. 150.
2. To earn a profit of 25%:
Selling Price (SP) per book = CP * (1 + 0.25) = 150 * 1.25 = Rs. 187.50.
Therefore, the selling price per copy should be Rs. 187.50.
