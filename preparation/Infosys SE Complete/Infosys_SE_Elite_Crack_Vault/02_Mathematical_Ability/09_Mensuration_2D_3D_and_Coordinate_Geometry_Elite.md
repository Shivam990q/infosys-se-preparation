   $$\text{Area} = 22 \times 0.5 \times 45.5 = 11 \times 45.5 = 500.5\text{ m}^2$$

**Question Statement:**

The parallel sides of an isosceles trapezium are $20\text{ cm}$ and $10\text{ cm}$. Each of the non-parallel oblique sides is $13\text{ cm}$. What is the total area of the trapezium?

### Options
- a.  $150\text{ cm}^2$
- b.  $180\text{ cm}^2$
- c.  $210\text{ cm}^2$
- d.  $240\text{ cm}^2$

### Correct Answer
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Verified Answer Key**: `B. 180 cm²`

### Step-by-Step Mathematical Proof & Arithmetic Solution
1. **Determining Height of Trapezium ($h$):**
   In an isosceles trapezium with parallel sides $a = 20\text{ cm}$ and $b = 10\text{ cm}$, dropping perpendiculars from the top corners to the base leaves two symmetric right triangles with base:
   $$x = \frac{a - b}{2} = \frac{20 - 10}{2} = 5\text{ cm}$$
2. **Applying Pythagorean Theorem for Height ($h$):**
   The hypotenuse is the non-parallel side $= 13\text{ cm}$.
   $$h = \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{169 - 25} = \sqrt{144} = 12\text{ cm}$$
3. **Trapezium Area Formula:**
   $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times (a + b) \times h$$
   $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times (20 + 10) \times 12 = 15 \times 12 = 180\text{ cm}^2$$
   Therefore, the area of the trapezium is **180 cm²**. Verified Answer is **B**.
