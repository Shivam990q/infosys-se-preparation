## 🎯 1. Official Test Pattern & Sectional Timing Architecture

## 🔬 2. Exhaustive Topic Breakdown & Analytics

### A. Technical Ability (Mathematical Aptitude) — 10 Qs | 35 Mins
- **Percentages & Successive Change:** Population formula, expenditure constancy, successive shifts (0–1 Qs, Med).
- **Data Interpretation:** Tables, bar charts, pie graphs, radar diagrams (0–1 Qs, Med).
- **Permutation & Combination:** Selection constraints, circular arrangement, letter rearrangements (1 Q, High).
- **Probability:** Dice, cards, conditional probability, complementary events (0–1 Q, Med).
- **Areas, Shapes, Perimeter (Mensuration):** 2D/3D shapes, percentage area change (0–1 Q, High).
- **Speed Time & Distance / Boats & Streams:** Relative speed, trains crossing platforms/poles (1 Q, Med).
- **Time & Work / Pipes & Cisterns:** Alternate days, efficiency ratios, tank leaks (1 Q, High).
- **Profit & Loss / Mixtures & Alligations:** MP-CP relation, repeated dilutions, false weights (1 Q, High).
- **Problem on Ages:** Linear equations, invariant difference across time (0–1 Q, Med).
- **Divisibility Rules & Remainder Theorems:** Cyclicity of powers, prime factorization (1–2 Qs, High).
- **Number Decimal & Fractions / LCM & HCF:** Recurring decimals, BODMAS rules (0–1 Q, Med).

### B. Reasoning Ability (Logical Reasoning) — 15 Qs | 25 Mins
- **Syllogisms:** Deductive logic, 2–3 statements, either-or cases, possibility cases (1–2 Qs, Med–High).
- **Arrangements:** Circular (facing centre/outside) & linear multi-variable rows (0–1 Qs, Med).
- **Coding-Decoding:** Letter shifts, opposite pairs (A-Z), message deciphering (1–2 Qs, Med).
- **Number & Letter Series:** Step differences, difference of differences, power sequences (1–2 Qs, High).
- **Cryptarithmetic:** Letter-digit arithmetic mapping, carry rules (1–2 Qs, Med).
- **Clocks and Calendar:** Angle formula |30H - 5.5M|, odd days calculation (1–2 Qs, Med).
- **Data Sufficiency:** Evaluating sufficiency of 2 independent statements (1–2 Qs, Med).
- **Logical Deduction & Most Logical Choice:** Practical policy decision-making (2–3 Qs, Med).

### C. Verbal Ability (English) — 20 Qs | 20 Mins
- **Reading Comprehension:** 1 Long or 2 Short passages, inference, primary purpose, tone (1–2 Passages, Hard).
- **Sentence Correction & Spotting Errors:** Subject-verb agreement, parallelism, dangling modifiers (2–3 Qs, Med).
- **Sentence Completion & Fill in the Blanks:** Prepositions, tenses, vocabulary context (1–2 Qs, Easy–Med).
- **Para Jumbles:** Sentence rearrangement, opening sentence, mandatory pairs (1–2 Qs, Med).
- **One Word Substitution & Analogies:** Word relationship bridges, vocabulary (2–3 Qs, Med).

### D. Pseudo Code — 5 Qs | 10 Mins
- **Bitwise Operations:** AND (&), OR (|), XOR (^), Left Shift (<<), Right Shift (>>).
- **Loop Tracing:** Nested while/for loops, boundary conditions, accumulator variables.
- **Recursion:** Call stack execution, base condition returns, exponential recursion traces.
- **Data Structures Basics:** Arrays, pointers, string character frequency.

### E. Puzzle Solving — 4 Qs | 10 Mins
- **Number Grid / Matrix Puzzles:** Row/Column constant sums, factor relationships.
- **Geometric / Vertex Puzzles:** Outer vertices combining into inner central number.
- **Logical Riddles:** Matchstick, balance scales, truth/liar logic puzzles.

## 💡 3. Rules to Keep in Mind (PrepInsta Expert Guidelines)
1. **Never Skip Without Attempting:** Infosys has **zero negative marking**. Always select an option before time expires.
2. **Pacing in Technical Ability:** With 35 minutes for 10 questions, you have 3.5 minutes per question—do not rush; derive with precision.
3. **Pacing in Verbal Ability:** With 20 minutes for 20 questions, you have only 60 seconds per question—scan for subject-verb agreement and eliminate aggressively.
4. **High Value of Puzzles:** The 4 puzzle questions carry 2 to 3 marks each, creating a major differentiator in qualifying for the interview round.
