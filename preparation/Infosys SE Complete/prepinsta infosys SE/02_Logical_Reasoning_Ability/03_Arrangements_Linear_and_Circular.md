## 📋 Section 1: Rules to Keep in Mind
These rules and fundamental principles govern all Infosys questions on this topic:

- Circular Seating Facing Centre: Left is Clockwise, Right is Counter-Clockwise.
- Circular Seating Facing Outside: Left is Counter-Clockwise, Right is Clockwise.
- Linear Seating Facing North: Left is towards your left hand, Right is towards your right hand.
- Linear Seating Facing South: Left and Right directions invert.
- 'Immediate Left/Right' means adjacent position with no one in between; 'Second to the left' means 1 person between them.
