## 📋 Section 1: Rules to Keep in Mind
These rules and fundamental principles govern all Infosys questions on this topic:

- Universal Affirmative (A): All S are P (100% distribution of S).
- Universal Negative (E): No S is P (100% distribution of both S and P).
- Particular Affirmative (I): Some S are P (neither distributed).
- Particular Negative (O): Some S are not P (P distributed).
- Rules of Deduction: Two negative premises yield no valid conclusion; Two particular premises yield no valid conclusion.
- Either-Or Condition: Requires two complementary conclusions with same subjects and predicates (Some + No OR All + Some Not), where neither follows individually.

### Question 1 (PrepInsta quiz-1 - Q1)
**Question Stem:**
Directions: In the following question two statements are given and these statements are followed by two conclusions numbered (1) and (2). You have to take the given two statements to be true even if they seem to be at variance from commonly known facts. Read the conclusions and then decide which of the given conclusions logically follows from the two given statements, disregarding commonly known facts. 

   Statements: 

   Some papers are pens. 

   All pencils are pens. 

   Conclusions: 

   1. Some pens are pencils. 

   2. Some pens are papers.

**Options:**
- **(A)** `Only (1) conclusion follows`
- **(B)** `Only (2) conclusion follows`
- **(C)** `Both (1) and (2) follow`
- **(D)** `Either (1) or (2) follows`
- **(E)** `Neither (1) nor (2) follows`

**PrepInsta Detailed Explanation & Mathematical Derivation:**
```text
Tick &amp; cross Method :


Conclusions: 1. Some pens are pencils. - Possible - Because All pencils are pens.


Conclusions: 2. Some pens are papers. - Possible - Because Some papers are pens.


OR


Note: Here, a conclusion is definite if it can be shown in a diagram drawn with least-possibilities. If a conclusion can’t be shown in least-possibilities diagram then the conclusion is possible but not definite.


On drawing least-possibilities Venn-diagram:


Conclusions:


1. Some pens are pencils. ⇒ It’s, definitely, true.


2. Some pens are papers. ⇒ It’s, definitely, true.


Hence, both 1 and 2 follow
```

### Question 2 (PrepInsta quiz-1 - Q2)
**Question Stem:**
All birds are animals.   

 

 All animals are four legged. 

    Implications:  

   a. All animals which are four legged are birds. 

   b. All birds are four legged 

   c. Some birds not are four legged 

   d. Some birds are animals but not four legged.

**Options:**
- **(A)** `a and b`
- **(B)** `b and c`
- **(C)** `only b`
- **(D)** `only d`
- **(E)** `None is true`

**PrepInsta Detailed Explanation & Mathematical Derivation:**
```text
From the given conditions we draw the following least possible venn diagram:


Conclusions: Using tick &amp; cross.


a. All animals which are four legged are birds. (False as it is not definite)


b. All birds are four legged.  (Clearly true as it is definite taking middle term as animal)


c. Some birds are not four legged (False as it is not definite also no negative statement.)


d. Some birds are animals but not four legged (False as it is not definite also if all statements are positive then conclusion can never be negative)


Thus, conclusion (b) is true.
```
