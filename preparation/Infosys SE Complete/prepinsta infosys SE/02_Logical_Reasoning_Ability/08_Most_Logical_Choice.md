## 📋 Section 1: Rules to Keep in Mind
These governing corporate principles dictate the correct answer in all Infosys decision-making questions:

- **Rule of Professional Escalation:** Escalate risks transparently through established organizational hierarchies before issues become production crises.
- **Rule of Root-Cause Remediation:** Prefer solutions that identify and fix underlying systemic causes over quick-fix symptomatic patches.
- **Rule of Non-Escalation:** Avoid punitive, confrontational, or aggressive responses in workplace conflict scenarios.
- **Rule of Policy & Compliance Integrity:** Actions that compromise code quality, data security, client confidentiality, or contractual SLA terms are always INVALID.

## ⚡ Section 2: How to Solve Quickly
Proven time-saving strategies recommended by PrepInsta for Infosys 2026 examination:

1. **Eliminate Extreme / Punitive Options:** Any choice recommending immediate firing, public reprimand, or hostile confrontation is false in 100% of Infosys questions.
2. **Eliminate Unauthorized Actions:** Any choice suggesting secretive code changes, bypassing testing, or working around governance without approvals is wrong.
3. **Select Balanced, Proactive Solutions:** Look for options featuring documentation, clear communication, risk assessment, and collaborative problem-solving.

## 🚀 Section 3: Tricks & Shortcuts
Key mathematical formulas, mental-math shortcuts, and speed axioms:

- **The 3C Matrix:** Correct choices always exhibit **C**ollaboration, **C**ompliance, and **C**larity.
- **Stakeholder Balance Test:** If an option solves an internal team problem but harms the client, or pleases the client but destroys code quality, it is sub-optimal.

### Question 1 (Infosys Most Logical Choice Scenario - 1)
**Context / Scenario:**
You are a software engineer at Infosys assigned to a critical banking client project. Two days before production deployment, you discover a subtle boundary-condition bug that could cause inaccurate interest calculations under rare leap-year circumstances. The module was already signed off by the QA lead.

**Question Stem:**
Which of the following represents the Most Logical Choice of action?

**Options:**
- `(A) Ignore the bug since QA already signed off and it only affects rare leap-year scenarios.`
- `(B) Secretly hotfix the code directly in production after deployment without informing anyone.`
- `(C) Document the bug, assess the business risk, and immediately inform your Module Lead and QA Lead with a proposed patch for review before production deployment.`
- `(D) Refuse to proceed with deployment and publicly blame the QA team in the client status meeting.`

**Correct Answer:** `(C) Document the bug, assess the business risk, and immediately inform your Module Lead and QA Lead with a proposed patch for review before production deployment.`

**Detailed Business Logic & Corporate Derivation:**
```text
Corporate decision-making principle: Transparent risk escalation with documented remediation. Choice C balances financial client integrity, collaborative teamwork, and proactive problem-solving without reckless unauthorized changes or destructive finger-pointing.
```

### Question 2 (Infosys Most Logical Choice Scenario - 2)
**Context / Scenario:**
During an ongoing team sprint, a team member frequently misses scheduled daily stand-ups and delivers incomplete code modules, causing dependency bottlenecks for other developers.

**Question Stem:**
What is the Most Logical Course of action for the team?

**Options:**
- `(A) Exclude the member from the project and distribute their workload among the remaining developers.`
- `(B) Privately communicate with the team member to understand the root cause of the delay, offer necessary technical mentoring, and mutually agree on realistic milestone check-ins while keeping the project manager informed.`
- `(C) Report the teammate directly to senior HR demanding disciplinary action without prior discussion.`
- `(D) Ignore the missed stand-ups and delay the entire project delivery date.`

**Correct Answer:** `(B) Privately communicate with the team member to understand the root cause of the delay, offer necessary technical mentoring, and mutually agree on realistic milestone check-ins while keeping the project manager informed.`

**Detailed Business Logic & Corporate Derivation:**
```text
Root-cause remediation and empathetic professional peer communication. Resolves the issue constructively while maintaining delivery discipline.
```

### Question 3 (Infosys Most Logical Choice Scenario - 3)
**Context / Scenario:**
A client requests an out-of-scope feature to be implemented immediately without logging a formal Change Request (CR) or extending the project deadline, claiming that failing to do so will harm the relationship.

**Question Stem:**
Which response represents the Most Logical Choice?

**Options:**
- `(A) Accept the request verbally and force your developers to work unpaid overtime to deliver it.`
- `(B) Flatly refuse the client's request without explaining the organizational process.`
- `(C) Politely acknowledge the business value of the feature, explain the project impact on scope, schedule, and quality, and guide the client through the standard Change Request (CR) process with an estimated cost and timeline.`
- `(D) Implement half of the feature poorly to appease the client.`

**Correct Answer:** `(C) Politely acknowledge the business value of the feature, explain the project impact on scope, schedule, and quality, and guide the client through the standard Change Request (CR) process with an estimated cost and timeline.`

**Detailed Business Logic & Corporate Derivation:**
```text
Professional client stakeholder management adheres to scope control while preserving client relationships through constructive, formal governance.
```
