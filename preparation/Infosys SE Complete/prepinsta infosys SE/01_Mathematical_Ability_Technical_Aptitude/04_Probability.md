## 📋 Section 1: Rules to Keep in Mind
These rules and fundamental principles govern all Infosys questions on this topic:

- Probability P(E) = Number of Favourable Outcomes / Total Number of Elementary Outcomes.
- 0 <= P(E) <= 1; P(E) + P(not E) = 1.
- Addition Theorem: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B). For mutually exclusive events: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B).
- Independent Events: P(A and B) = P(A) * P(B).
- Conditional Probability: P(A | B) = P(A and B) / P(B).
