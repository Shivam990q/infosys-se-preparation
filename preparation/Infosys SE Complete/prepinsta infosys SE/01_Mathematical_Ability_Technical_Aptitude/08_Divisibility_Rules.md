## 📋 Section 1: Rules to Keep in Mind
These rules and fundamental principles govern all Infosys questions on this topic:

- Divisibility by 2: Last digit is even; by 4: Last 2 digits divisible by 4; by 8: Last 3 digits divisible by 8.
- Divisibility by 3: Sum of digits divisible by 3; by 9: Sum of digits divisible by 9.
- Divisibility by 5: Last digit 0 or 5; by 10: Last digit 0.
- Divisibility by 11: Difference between sum of odd-positioned digits and even-positioned digits is 0 or multiple of 11.
- Divisibility by composite numbers (e.g. 72): Number must be divisible by co-prime factors 8 and 9.

## 🚀 Section 3: Tricks & Shortcuts
Key mathematical formulas, mental-math shortcuts, and speed axioms:

- Wilson's & Fermat's Little Theorem: a^(p-1) mod p = 1 where p is prime and gcd(a, p) = 1.
- Unit digit of large powers cyclicity: 2, 3, 7, 8 have cyclicity 4; 4, 9 have cyclicity 2; 0, 1, 5, 6 have cyclicity 1.

### Question 4 (PrepInsta quiz-1 - Q4)
**Question Stem:**
When the integer n is divided by 8, the remainder is 3. What is the remainder if 6n is divided by 8?

**Options:**
- **(A)** `1`
- **(B)** `2`
- **(C)** `3`
- **(D)** `4`

**Correct Answer:** **(B)** `2`

**PrepInsta Detailed Explanation & Mathematical Derivation:**
```text
When n is divided by 8, the remainder is 3 may be written as 


n = 8 k + 3


multiply all terms by 6 


6 n = 6(8 k + 3) = 8(6k) + 18


Write 18 as 16 + 2 since 16 = 8 * 2.


= 8(6k) + 16 + 2


Factor 8 out.


= 8(6k + 2) + 2


The above indicates that if 6n is divided by 8, the remainder is 2. The answer is B.
```
