## 📋 Section 1: Rules to Keep in Mind
These rules and fundamental principles govern all Infosys questions on this topic:

- Percentage to fraction conversion: x% = x/100; Fraction to percentage: a/b = (a/b) * 100%.
- Percentage Increase = [(Final - Initial) / Initial] * 100%.
- Percentage Decrease = [(Initial - Final) / Initial] * 100%.
- If A is r% more than B, then B is [r / (100 + r)] * 100% less than A.
- If A is r% less than B, then B is [r / (100 - r)] * 100% more than A.
- Successive percentage change of a% and b% = [a + b + (ab/100)]%.

## 🚀 Section 3: Tricks & Shortcuts
Key mathematical formulas, mental-math shortcuts, and speed axioms:

- Standard fractions: 1/2=50%, 1/3=33.33%, 1/4=25%, 1/5=20%, 1/6=16.66%, 1/7=14.28%, 1/8=12.5%, 1/9=11.11%, 1/11=9.09%, 1/12=8.33%.
- Product Constancy Law: If Price increases by 1/n, Consumption must decrease by 1/(n+1) to keep expenditure constant.
- Population after n years: P * (1 + r/100)^n; Population n years ago: P / (1 + r/100)^n.
