## 📋 Section 1: Rules to Keep in Mind
These rules and fundamental principles govern all Infosys questions on this topic:

- Profit = SP - CP; Loss = CP - SP.
- Profit % = (Profit / CP) * 100; Loss % = (Loss / CP) * 100 (Always calculated on Cost Price).
- SP = CP * (100 + P%) / 100; CP = [100 / (100 + P%)] * SP.
- Discount is always calculated on Marked Price (MP): Discount = MP - SP; Discount % = (Discount / MP) * 100.
- SP = MP * (100 - D%) / 100.

## 🚀 Section 3: Tricks & Shortcuts
Key mathematical formulas, mental-math shortcuts, and speed axioms:

- Relation between MP and CP: (MP / CP) = (100 + P%) / (100 - D%).
- When two articles are sold at the same selling price, one at a gain of x% and the other at a loss of x%, there is always an overall loss of (x/10)^2 %.
- Dishonest dealer using false weight: Gain % = [Error / (True Value - Error)] * 100%.
