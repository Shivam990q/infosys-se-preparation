## 📋 Section 1: Rules to Keep in Mind
These rules and fundamental principles govern all Infosys questions on this topic:

- If A can complete a work in n days, A's 1 day work = 1/n.
- Total Work = Efficiency * Time.
- Combined work of A and B: 1/Time = 1/A + 1/B => Time = (A * B) / (A + B).
- Pipes and Cisterns: Inlet pipe rate is positive (+1/x); Outlet / leak pipe rate is negative (-1/y).
- Net rate = 1/x - 1/y.

## 🚀 Section 3: Tricks & Shortcuts
Key mathematical formulas, mental-math shortcuts, and speed axioms:

- LCM Method for Total Work: Assume total work = LCM of individual times; find daily unit efficiency.
- Chain Rule: (M1 * D1 * H1) / W1 = (M2 * D2 * H2) / W2.
- Efficiency is inversely proportional to time taken: Ratio of efficiency A:B = y:x if time ratio is x:y.
