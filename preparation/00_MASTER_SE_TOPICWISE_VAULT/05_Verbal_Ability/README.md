## ⚡ Master Verbal Rules & High-Yield Grammar Points

### 1. Subject-Verb Agreement Essentials
* **"The number of" vs "A number of"**:
  * *The number of* applicants **is** increasing. (Singular verb)
  * *A number of* applicants **have** been shortlisted. (Plural verb)
* **Compound Subjects with "Neither...nor" / "Either...or"**:
  * Verb agrees with the subject **closest** to it: *Neither the manager nor the employees were present.*
* **Parenthetical Phrases**: Phrases like *along with, together with, as well as, in addition to* do not change the number of the subject: *The CEO, along with his directors, **is** attending.*

### 2. Conditionals & Inversions
* **Third Conditional (Past Unreal)**: *If + had + V3, would have + V3*.
  * Inverted form: *Had they approved the proposal earlier, the implementation would have succeeded.*
* **Negative Inversion**: When a negative adverb begins a sentence (*Rarely, Seldom, Never, Scarcely, Hardly, No sooner*), use inverted word order: *Seldom **have I seen** such dedication.*

### 3. Idiom & Vocabulary Traps
* **A red herring**: Something that misleads or distracts from a relevant or important question.
* **A hot potato**: A controversial issue that is awkward or unpleasant to deal with.
* **Bite the bullet**: To face a difficult situation with courage and fortitude.
