### 3. Key Sectional Strategies & Proven Cutoff Thresholds
- **Reasoning Section:** Do not spend more than 2 minutes on complex blood relation or seating puzzles. Use elimination for Syllogisms.
- **Quantitative Section:** 35 minutes for 10 questions is generous (3.5 min/Q). Use this time for rigorous verification of permutation edge cases and CI/SI equations.
- **Verbal Section:** Read the 5 questions BEFORE scanning the RC passage. Skim for target keywords (e.g. Goodhart's law, idempotency, student syndrome).
- **Pseudo Code:** Never guess. Always maintain a tabular execution trace tracking variable values across each iteration.
- **Writing Section:** Adhere strictly to the word limit (120–150 words for emails, 150–180 words for essays). Follow the standard business structure: Salutation -> Direct Context -> Technical Justification -> Action Plan -> Professional Sign-off.
