Question 2

Arjun is older than Sumit but younger than Rahul. Vikas is younger than Rahul but older than Arjun. Ramesh is the oldest among them. Who is the third oldest?

  • (A)

    Sumit

  • (B)

    Arjun

  • (C)

    Rahul

  • (D)

    Vikas

    CORRECT
Correct Answer: D
Explanation:

From the given conditions:

Arjun > Sumit, but Arjun < Rahul.

→ Rahul > Arjun > Sumit.

Vikas is younger than Rahul but older than Arjun.

→ Rahul > Vikas > Arjun > Sumit.

Ramesh is the oldest.

Final order: Ramesh > Rahul > Vikas > Arjun > Sumit.

The third oldest is Vikas.

Question 3

Six people—P, Q, R, S, T, and U—are standing in a line from left to right. P is taller than S but shorter than Q. R is taller than Q but shorter than U. T is the tallest. Who is the fourth tallest?

  • (A)

    P

  • (B)

    Q

    CORRECT
  • (C)

    R

  • (D)

    S

Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

From the given conditions:

P > S, but P < Q → Q > P > S.

R > Q, but R < U → U > R > Q.

T is the tallest.

Final order: T > U > R > Q > P > S.

The fourth tallest person is Q.

Question 9

In a north-facing row of 45 students, Neha is 13th from the left, and Priya is 15th from the right. There are 4 students between Priya and Rani, who is equidistant to Neha. What is Rani’s position from the right?

  • (A)

    25

  • (B)

    20

    CORRECT
  • (C)

    30

  • (D)

    32

Correct Answer: B
Explanation:

Priya’s position from the left = 45−15+1=31.

Rani’s position from the left (5 places ahead of Priya) = 31−5=26.

Check equidistance: ∣26−13∣=13 (same as Neha's position). 

Rani’s position from the right = 45−26+1=20.

Question 12

If the rank of Rohan is 7th from the top in a group of 50 candidates and 2 new candidates are added just before and after him, what will be his new rank from bottom?

  • (A)

    44

  • (B)

    45

  • (C)

    46

    CORRECT
  • (D)

    48

Correct Answer: C
Explanation:

Adding of candidates just before Rohan will not change his rank from bottom. Before addition, the rank of Rohan from bottom is = (50 - 7) + 1 = 44

Now adding 2 candidates just after Rohan will increase his rank by 2 positions. Hence; the new rank of Rohan from bottom will be = 44 + 2 = 46

Question 20

In a class of 35 students, Ziya is placed 7th from the bottom where as Sofia is placed 9th from the top. Shahruk is placed in between the two. What is Ziya's position from Shahruk?

  • (A)

    10

    CORRECT
  • (B)

    15

  • (C)

    19

  • (D)

    21

Correct Answer: A
Explanation:

As seen in the figure, Shahruk is between Sofia and Ziya. It's given that Ziya is 7th from the bottom and Sofia is 9th from the top.

Therefore, number of persons between Sofia and Ziya = 35-(9+7) = 19

Sharuk's position between Sofia and Ziya = 19-1/2 = 10.

Hence, Shahruk is at the middle ie. at 10th position from both. Ziya, therefore, is at the 10th position from Shahruk
