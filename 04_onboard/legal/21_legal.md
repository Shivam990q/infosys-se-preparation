## 3. National Skills Registry (NSR) & ITPIN Generation

The **National Skills Registry (NSR)** is a secure, centralized database hosted by **NASSCOM** to verify credentialing across the Indian IT sector. Infosys mandates an active **ITPIN (IT Professional Identification Number)** prior to joining.

### Step-by-Step NSR Registration Workflow:

1. **Online Form Submission:**
   - Visit the official NSR portal: `https://nationalskillsregistry.com`
   - Select **"Register for NSR"** as a New Professional.
   - Enter Personal Details, Educational History (10th, 12th, B.Tech/B.E.), and Identity Proof (PAN / Aadhaar).
   - Enter **Infosys Limited** as your employing/sponsoring organization.

2. **Fee Payment & Acknowledgement Slip:**
   - Pay the registration fee (~INR 400 + GST for 1 year or lifetime profile).
   - Download the generated **Acknowledgement Form** containing your **Web-PIN** (Temporary Tracking Number).

3. **Biometric Capture (E-KYC / Point of Service):**
   - Option A (Aadhaar Online E-KYC): Authenticate via Aadhaar OTP and biometric token directly on the portal.
   - Option B (Physical POS Visit): Visit an authorized NSR Point of Service (POS) center with your Acknowledgement Slip and photo ID for fingerprint capture.

4. **ITPIN Generation:**
   - Once biometric authentication completes, a permanent **10-digit alphanumeric ITPIN** is issued.
   - You must copy this 10-digit ITPIN and paste it into the designated field on the **Infosys Launchpad Onboarding Dashboard**.

## 4. Forensic Legal Analysis of the Infosys Service Agreement & Bond

Upon joining, every Systems Engineer trainee signs a **Service Agreement (Bond)** backed by a personal surety (guarantor).

### Key Terms of the Service Agreement:
- **Duration:** **12 Months (1 Year)** from the Date of Joining (DOJ).
- **Stated Liquidated Damages:** **₹1,00,000 (INR 1 Lakh) + ₹25,000 (Training Cost / GST)** = **₹1,25,000 total**.
- **Surety Requirement:** Requires a salaried guarantor (non-blood or family depending on year) who signs on a non-judicial stamp paper with valid proof of income (Form 16 or salary slip).

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                       LEGAL STANDING UNDER INDIAN LAW                           |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Governing Statute: Section 27 and Section 74 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872   |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1. Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 (Restraint of Trade)
- **The Law:** *"Every agreement by which any one is restrained from exercising a lawful profession, trade or business of any kind, is to that extent void."*
- **Judicial Position:** The Supreme Court of India (*Percept D'Mark v. Zaheer Khan, 2006*) and various High Courts have consistently ruled that **no employer can physically or legally force an employee to work against their will**. Bonded labor is prohibited under Article 23 of the Constitution of India.

### 2. Section 74 of the Indian Contract Act (Liquidated Damages vs Penalty)
- **The Rule:** An employer cannot levy arbitrary "penalties" on an employee who resigns before completing a bond term.
- **Enforceable Claim:** An employer is only legally entitled to recover **actual, provable, direct expenses incurred** on training the employee (e.g., accommodation costs at Mysore, trainer salaries, specialized course software licenses).
- **Corporate Reality:** If an engineer resigns during the 1-year bond period:
  - If the engineer pays the prorated bond amount or negotiates exit settlement, Infosys formally issues the **Relieving Letter** and **Service Certificate**.
  - If an engineer absconds without paying or serving notice, Infosys will **withhold the Relieving Letter and Service Certificate**, mark the candidate's NSR profile as non-compliant, and send a legal recovery demand notice to the surety.
  - *Recommendation:* Always serve the formal notice period and obtain the official Relieving Letter; joining subsequent companies without an authentic Relieving Letter leads to instant disqualification in future BGV checks.

## 5. The Infosys Non-Compete Clause: Facts vs Reality

In 2022, Infosys faced significant public scrutiny regarding a clause in its offer letter:

> *"For a period of 6 months following termination of employment, the employee shall not accept any offer of employment from any customer where they provided services in the preceding 12 months, nor accept employment from designated competitors (TCS, Wipro, Cognizant, Accenture, HCL) if it involves working on the same customer account."*

### Legal Truth:
1. **Restraint of Trade Voidness:** Under Section 27, any post-termination non-compete clause is legally unenforceable in Indian courts. An employee is free to work for any competitor.
2. **Narrow Scope:** The clause was never a blanket ban on joining TCS or Wipro; it was an account-specific covenant designed to prevent poaching of direct client accounts.
3. **Current Freshers Status:** For entry-level Systems Engineers (JL2 / JL3), Infosys does not enforce non-compete restrictions against joining competing IT services companies upon proper completion of the 90-day notice period.
