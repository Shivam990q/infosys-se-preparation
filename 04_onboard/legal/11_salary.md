## 3. Service Agreement (Bond) Forensics

### Key Terms & Legal Reality
- **Bond Penalty Amount:** Typically **₹1,00,000 to ₹1,25,000**.
- **Bond Duration:** **12 months (1 year)** starting from the formal date of joining.
- **Legal Enforceability:** Under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, negative covenants restricting trade are generally void. However, Indian courts have upheld that companies can recover reasonable, documented training expenses incurred on an employee.
- **Operational Consequences of Leaving Early:**
  1. Infosys will **not issue your Experience Letter, Relieving Letter, or Service Certificate**.
  2. Your Employee ID will be marked as "Not Eligible for Rehire" in Infosys's internal HR database.
  3. Third-party Background Verification (BGV) checks by future employers will return an adverse finding if you list your Infosys tenure without authentic relieving documentation.
- **Best Practice:** Complete the 12-month service period or formally settle the exit dues with HR.
