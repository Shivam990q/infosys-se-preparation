## 2. Infosys Springboard: The Secret to Accelerating Your DOJ

Infosys Springboard is the official enterprise digital learning platform provided to candidates before onboarding.

### How Springboard Directly Impacts Your Career:
1. **Wave Prioritization:** Infosys's automated allocation algorithms prioritize candidates who complete their assigned Springboard pre-requisite courses for **Wave 1 (July/August onboarding)**.
2. **Fast-Track Foundation Program (FP):** Trainees who score $>80\%$ in the Springboard pre-induction assessments are eligible to sit for the **Fast-Track Mysore Assessment** during Day 1 induction. Passing this allows you to **skip 4–6 weeks of generic training**, saving time and reducing stress.
3. **Smooth Mysore Experience:** The Python, SQL, and OOPs modules on Springboard are identical to the actual FA1 and FA2 Mysore curriculum. Completing them in advance guarantees you will not struggle or face retest termination.

## 5. What to Do If Your DOJ Is Delayed Beyond 6 Months

If you receive an offer but hear nothing for 4–5 months:

1. **Do NOT Spam HR Daily:** Excessive emails to individual HR managers trigger automated support ticket closures.
2. **Official Query Route:** Raise a formal inquiry through the **Infosys Launchpad Helpdesk** or contact your **College Placement Officer (TPO)**, who holds a direct nodal line with the Regional Talent Acquisition Head.
3. **Active Upskilling:** Continue solving problems on LeetCode and building full-stack projects. When you arrive at Mysore with advanced skills, you can easily clear the **Bridge to DSE/SP** assessment and jump directly from ₹3.60 LPA to **₹6.25 LPA or ₹9.50 LPA**.
