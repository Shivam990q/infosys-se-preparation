## 2. Health Insurance & Medical Benefits (Mediclaim)

- **Employee & Immediate Family Floater:** Infosys provides a base group health insurance (Mediclaim) policy of **₹4,00,000** covering the employee, spouse, and up to 2 children.
- **Parental Insurance Scheme:** Employees can enroll their parents and parents-in-law under a corporate group policy at subsidized premium rates deducted directly from monthly payroll.
- **On-Campus Clinics & First Aid:** Major development centers (Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Mysore) feature 24/7 on-campus Apollo / Fortis medical clinics with free doctor consultations and emergency ambulance facilities.

## 3. Higher Education Policy (BITS Pilani WILP M.Tech)

Infosys supports employees pursuing higher education while working through its **Higher Education Section (HES)**:

- **Flagship Program:** **BITS Pilani Work Integrated Learning Programmes (WILP) — M.Tech in Software Systems / Computing Systems / AI & Data Science**.
- **Mode of Learning:** Weekend interactive virtual lectures and evening self-paced labs designed specifically for working software engineers.
- **Fee Reimbursement Policy:**
  - Infosys reimburses up to **50% of the tuition fees** (subject to an internal cap, historically up to ₹1,00,000) upon successful semester-by-semester completion.
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  1. Minimum **1 year of continuous service** as a confirmed Systems Engineer (JL3).
  2. Consistent performance rating of **"Meets Expectations" (ME)** or higher in annual appraisals.
  3. Manager and Delivery Unit (DU) approval.
  4. A service commitment (retention agreement) to serve the company for a specified period (typically 1–2 years) following degree completion.

## 4. Leave Policy & Paid Time Off (PTO)

For junior engineers and Systems Engineers (JL2 / JL3):
- **Earned Leave (EL):** **15 days per year**, credited quarterly as **4 – 4 – 4 – 3 days** at the start of each quarter.
- **Carry-Forward Rule:** You can carry forward up to **50% of unused Earned Leaves** to the subsequent calendar year, up to a maximum cap. Unused leaves beyond the cap lapse or can be encashed during final exit.
- **National & Festival Holidays:** **10 declared public holidays per year** (varies slightly by state development center: Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, Tamil Nadu).
- **Maternity & Paternity Leave:** 26 weeks paid maternity leave; 5 days paid paternity leave.

## 6. Critical Compliance & Risk Policies (Must-Know)

### 1. Zero-Tolerance Moonlighting Policy
Infosys strictly prohibits employees from engaging in any secondary employment, commercial freelance work, or external business ventures while employed:
- **How it is detected:** The Ministry of Labour's **Universal Account Number (UAN)** automatically flags dual Provident Fund (PF) deposits from multiple employers under the same PAN/Aadhaar.
- **Penalty:** Immediate termination of employment with an adverse record in the National Skills Registry (NSR).

### 2. The Non-Compete Clause
Infosys employment contracts contain a non-compete covenant stipulating that for **6 months post-exit**, an employee cannot work for a direct competitor (e.g., TCS, Wipro, Cognizant, HCL, IBM) **on the exact same client account** that they serviced at Infosys:
- *Legal Reality:* Indian courts generally protect an employee's constitutional right to livelihood under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act. In practice, moving to another IT company is completely normal, provided you do not misappropriate proprietary client code, trade secrets, or client intellectual property.

### 3. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) & Bench Traps
- **What triggers a PIP?** Receiving a **"Below Expectations" (BE)** rating or failing 3 consecutive client/project interviews while unallocated on the bench.
- **Duration & Process:** A structured 30 to 60-day performance review with assigned technical deliverables. If the deliverables fail evaluation, the employee is asked to resign or is separated.
- **How to avoid PIP:** Never remain passive on the bench. Complete Lex certifications, take internal skill tags, and proactively contact project managers in your delivery unit.
